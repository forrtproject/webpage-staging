Replications

  • Intermodal Priming of Cognitive Conflict? : A Failed Replication of Mager et al. (2009), Wiswede & Rüsseler (2021). Outcome recorded: failed. The current study failed to replicate the astonishing concept of "conflict priming" reported in previous work and does not open the doors for a new window on sequences of conflicts.
