Replications

  • Do experimental asset market results replicate? High-powered preregistered replications of 17 claims, Huber et al. (2024). SSRN Electronic Journal. Outcome recorded: failed. We failed to replicate the focal results of AOL and KLS, hich had suggested that mispricing in asset markets is driven by emotions or low self-control, respectively, with replication effect sizes indistinguishable from zero.
