Replications

  • Does Measurement Affect the Gender Gap in Political Partisanship?, Krupnikov et al. (2021). Public Opinion Quarterly. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to Burden (2008), our data show that measures of partisanship that prime affect do not lead women to be more likely to identify as Republicans. Rather, these measures lead women of both parties to be more likely to identify as independent—a pattern that leaves the partisan gender gap in place, but suggests that both parties may be losing women.
