Working Hours and Carbon Dioxide Emissions in the United States, 2007–2013

Jared B Fitzgerald, Juliet B Schor, Andrew K Jorgenson

Social Forces, 2018. DOI 10.1093/sf/soy014

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • The relationship between state-level carbon emissions and average working hours in the United States: a replication study, Mallinson & Cheng (2021). Environmental Sociology. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated their analyses by including more recent data and confirmed the linkage between the average hours worked in a state and annual overall CO2 emissions. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Fitzgerald SocialForces 2018 4q0L - Cheng/Mallinson - Secondary Data Replication, Mallinson et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. The average hours worked is positively correlated with state CO2emissions in the United States(b= 0.535139, SE = 0.139448, p= 0.000143). Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
