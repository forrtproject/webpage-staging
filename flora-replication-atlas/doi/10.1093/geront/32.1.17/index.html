E. A. Nelson, D. Dannefer

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • The Continued Eclipse of Heterogeneity in Gerontological Research, Stone et al. (2017). The Journals of Gerontology Series B: Psychological Sciences and Social Sciences. Outcome recorded: successful. Indeed, comparison of our findings with those of Nelson and Dannefer (1992) suggests little change in the proportion of studies reporting variability (43% in Nelson and Dannefer's analysis and 42% in the current analysis) and an actual decrease in the proportion of articles discussing measures of age-based variability (39 out of 127 [31%] in Nelson and Dannefer's analysis and 13 out of 231 [6%] in the current analysis).
