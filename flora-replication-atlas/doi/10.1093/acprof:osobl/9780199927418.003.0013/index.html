Gender and Philosophical Intuition*

Wesley Buckwalter, Stephen Stich

Experimental Philosophy, 2014. DOI 10.1093/acprof:osobl/9780199927418.003.0013

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • On gender and philosophical intuition: Failure of replication and other negative results, Seyedsayamdost (2014). Philosophical Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. We failed to replicate the results using several different sources for data collection (one being identical to the original procedures). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Do men and women have different philosophical intuitions? Further data, Adleberg et al. (2014). Philosophical Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. Based on our findings, we argue that that it is unlikely that gender differences in intuitions play a significant role in driving women from philosophy. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
