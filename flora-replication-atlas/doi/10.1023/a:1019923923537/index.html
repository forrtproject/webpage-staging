Replications

  • Perceived morality of direct versus indirect harm: Replications of the preference for indirect harm effect, Ziano et al. (2021). Meta-Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. we successfully replicated Study 2 of Royzman and Baron (2002) with a Hong Kong student sample (N = 46) and an online American Mechanical Turk sample (N = 314). We found con- sistent evidential support for the preference for indirect harm phenomenon (d = 0.46 [0.26, 0.65] to 0.47 [0.18, 0.75]), weaker than effects reported in the original findings of the target article (d = 0.70 [0.40, 0.99]).
