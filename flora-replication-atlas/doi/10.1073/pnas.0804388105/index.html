Communication-induced memory biases in preverbal infants

Jennifer M. D. Yoon, Mark H. Johnson, Gergely Csibra

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 2008. DOI 10.1073/pnas.0804388105

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Probing communication-induced memory biases in preverbal infants: Two replication attempts of Yoon, Johnson and Csibra (2008), Silverstein et al. (2019). Infant Behavior and Development. Outcome recorded: failed. Experiment 1 was designed following the methods described in the original paper. After discussion with one of the original authors, some key changes were made to the methodology in Experiment 2. Neither experiment replicated the results of the original study, with Bayes Factor Analysis suggesting moderate support for the null hypothesis. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Probing Communication Induced Memory Biases in Preverbal Infants: Two Replication Attempts of Yoon, Johnson and Csibra (2008), unknown authors (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. Neither experiment replicated the results of the original study, with Bayes Factor Analysis suggesting moderate support for the null hypothesis. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
