Distinct patterns of brain activity in young carriers of the APOE -ε4 allele

Nicola Filippini, Bradley J. MacIntosh, Morgan G. Hough, Guy M. Goodwin, Giovanni B. Frisoni, Stephen M. Smith, Paul M. Matthews, Christian F. Beckmann, Clare E. Mackay

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 2009. DOI 10.1073/pnas.0811879106

Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • Functional co-activation of the default mode network in APOE ε4-carriers: A replication study, Mentink et al. (2021). NeuroImage. Outcome recorded: failed. Our primary analysis did not replicate the early findings of Filippini et al. (2009). However, compared with non-carriers, APOE ε4-carriers showed increased functional activation during the encoding of subsequently recollected items in areas related to facial recognition (p<0.05, t>756.11). This increased functional activation was also positively associated with PRS (APOE variants included) (p<0.05, t>647.55).
