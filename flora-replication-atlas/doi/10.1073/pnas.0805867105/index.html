Unconscious processing dissociates along categorical lines

Jorge Almeida, Bradford Z. Mahon, Ken Nakayama, Alfonso Caramazza

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 2008. DOI 10.1073/pnas.0805867105

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Does the Human Dorsal Stream Really Process a Category for Tools?, Sakuraba et al. (2012). The Journal of Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: statistically successful but flawed. Our results suggest that the dorsal stream processes elongated shapes but does not process the tool category specifically. Although Almeida et al. (2008) noted that dorsal stream computations influence object recognition processes for objects that can be manipulated, our results suggest that dorsal stream activation during priming may be due to shape attributes. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
