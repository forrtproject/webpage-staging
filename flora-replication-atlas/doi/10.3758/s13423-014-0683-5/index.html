Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • Registered Replication Report: A Large Multilab Cross-Cultural Conceptual Replication of Turri et al. (2015), Hall et al. (2024). Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. we did find evidence for Gettier intuitions; participants were 1.86 times more likely to attribute knowledge to protagonists in standard cases of JTB than to protagonists in Gettier-type cases.
