  • Exploring Memory Span and Capacity: A Replication of Martin (1978, Experiment 2), Garcia Mairena et al. (2025). The Quantitative Methods for Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. The results obtained in the current study of Martin's (1978) second experiment are consistent with the hypothesis that digit span is a reliable predictor of other memory tasks. However, our findings only partially align with those reported by Martin (1978).
