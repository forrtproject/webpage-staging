Replications

  • Inhibition of the mirror generalization process in reading in school-aged children, Ahr et al. (2016). Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. the current study aimed at replicating this result in school-aged children. Three age groups—1st, 3rd, and 5th graders— performed a negative priming study in which they were asked to determine on the primes whether two letters were identical and on the probes whether two animals facing opposite directions were identical.
