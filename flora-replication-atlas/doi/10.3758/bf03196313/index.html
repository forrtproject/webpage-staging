Grounding language in action

Arthur M. Glenberg, Michael P. Kaschak

Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 2002. DOI 10.3758/bf03196313

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • A pre-registered, multi-lab non-replication of the action-sentence compatibility effect (ACE), Morey et al. (2021). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. Outcome recorded: failed. none of the 18 labs involved in the study observed a reliable ACE, and that the meta-analytic estimate of the size of the ACE was essentially zero. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
