E2-2 Protein and Fuchs's Corneal Dystrophy

Keith H. Baratz, Nirubol Tosakulwong, Euijung Ryu, William L. Brown, Kari Branham, Wei Chen, Khoa D. Tran, Katharina E. Schmid-Kubista, John R. Heckenlively, Anand Swaroop, Goncalo Abecasis, Kent R. Bailey, Albert O. Edwards

New England Journal of Medicine, 2010. DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1007064

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Replication of the TCF4 Intronic Variant in Late-Onset Fuchs Corneal Dystrophy and Evidence of Independence from the FCD2 Locus, Riazuddin et al. (2011). Investigative Opthalmology & Visual Science. Outcome recorded: successful. "the first independent replication of rs613872 conferring risk of late-onset FCD." Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Replication of TCF4 through Association and Linkage Studies in Late-Onset Fuchs Endothelial Corneal Dystrophy, Li et al. (2011). PLoS ONE. Outcome recorded: mixed. Although we replicated the association of rs613872 with FECD in our dataset, we failed to replicate the association with rs10490775 in PTPRG . Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
