The relationships between behavioral addictions and the five-factor model of personality

Cecilie Schou Andreassen, Mark D. Griffiths, Siri Renate Gjertsen, Elfrid Krossbakken, Siri Kvam, Ståle Pallesen

Journal of Behavioral Addictions, 2013. DOI 10.1556/jba.2.2013.003

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Exploring the role of Honesty-Humility in replicating "The relationships between behavioral addictions and the five-factor model of personality", Singh & Durand (2022). The Quantitative Methods for Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. We partially replicated the findings of the original study. Our results support the role of personality, particularly conscientiousness, in predicting behavioral addictions, such as video game, Internet, and mobile phone addiction. However, we failed to replicate the same pattern of personality traits for each behavioral addiction. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
