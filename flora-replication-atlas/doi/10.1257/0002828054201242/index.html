Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Do rural banks matter that much? Burgess and Pande (2005) reconsidered, Buliskeria & Baxa (2022). Journal of Applied Econometrics. Outcome recorded: computationally reproducible, robustness challenges. Computational: First, we replicate the results obtained by Burgess and Pande (2005) successfully. We identify significant trend reversals in bank branch expansion and the effect of new branches openings on poverty using the 1977 cut-year when the bank licensing policy was supposed to be adopted according to authors.
