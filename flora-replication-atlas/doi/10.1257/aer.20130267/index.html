Jessica Cohen, Pascaline Dupas, Simone Schaner

American Economic Review, 2015. DOI 10.1257/aer.20130267

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 2 reproductions of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 mixed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications
