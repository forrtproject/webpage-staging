The Slave Trade and the Origins of Mistrust in Africa

Nathan Nunn, Leonard Wantchekon

American Economic Review, 2011. DOI 10.1257/aer.101.7.3221

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Modern Slavery and Mistrust: A Conceptual Replication of Nunn & Wantchekon (AER, 2011), Cao & Rusch (2025). Outcome recorded: successful. Our results corroborate those of Nunn & Wantchekon, conceptually replicate them in the economics of crime, and highlight their persistent contemporary relevance. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Narrow and scientific replication of 'The slave trade and the origins of mistrust in Africa', Deconinck & Verpoorten (2012). Journal of Applied Econometrics. Outcome recorded: successful. Our replication confirms the results of Nunn and Wantchekon. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • The Slave Trade and the Origins of Mistrust in Africa. A Replication Study of Nunn and Wantchekon (2011), Zanella (2025). Journal of Comments and Replications in Economics. Outcome recorded: mixed. While I can perfectly replicate the original OLS and 2SLS findings in this wave starting from the same raw data used by these authors, using data from more recent waves which were collected after the paper was published, I fail to replicate some of the paper's central findings. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
