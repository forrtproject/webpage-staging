Replications

  • Does it actually feel right? A replication attempt of the rounded price effect, Harms et al. (2018). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Our results did not unambiguously support the rounded price effect and provide evidence against the proposed mediation. While the descriptive statistics hint in the predicted direction, analyses using significance tests and Bayes factors could not yield strong evidence for the effect, except for a single contrast that was significant.
