Enhancing the Television-Viewing Experience through Commercial Interruptions

Leif D. Nelson, Tom Meyvis, Jeff Galak

Journal of Consumer Research, 2009. DOI 10.1086/597030

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Nelson JournConsRes 2009 eg1q - Chopik - New Data Replication, Leahy et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. "The replication of the claim was unsuccessful according to the SCORE criteria. Specifically, for stage 1 data collection (analytic N = 380), we found no significant difference between conditions. For stage 2 (with additional participants, analytic N = 1125), we found a significant difference between conditions in the opposite direction. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
