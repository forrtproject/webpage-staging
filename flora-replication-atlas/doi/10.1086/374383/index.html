Significant Linkage of Parkinson Disease to Chromosome 2q36-37

Nathan Pankratz, William C. Nichols, Sean K. Uniacke, Cheryl Halter, Alice Rudolph, Cliff Shults, P. Michael Conneally, Tatiana Foroud

The American Journal of Human Genetics, 2003. DOI 10.1086/374383

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2004.

Replications

  • PARK11 is not linked with Parkinson's disease in European families, Prestel et al. (2004). European Journal of Human Genetics. Outcome recorded: failed. Linkage analysis in 153 individuals from 45 European families with a strong family history of PD did not show any significant LOD score in this region. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
