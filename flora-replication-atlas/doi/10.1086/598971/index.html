The Presence of Variety Reduces Perceived Quantity

Joseph P. Redden, Stephen J. Hoch

Journal of Consumer Research, 2009. DOI 10.1086/598971

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • The Effect of Variety on Perceived Quantity, Röseler et al. (2024). Meta-Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. We conducted six studies with methods that were similar to those used by Redden and Hoch and did not find this effect in any of them. A meta-analysis of the four original studies and 6 replication studies (N = 1,383) revealed no evidence for the phenomenon that variety reduces perceived quantity. Outcome read from the abstract.
