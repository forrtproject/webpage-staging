Keisha M. Cutright, Eugenia C. Wu, Jillian C. Banfield, Aaron C. Kay, Gavan J. Fitzsimons

Journal of Consumer Research, 2011. DOI 10.1086/658469

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Cutright JournConsRes 2011 eg3p - Baskin - New Data Replication, Baskin et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. "The key analysis is a logistic regression with tshirt choice (national versus international choice) as the dependent variable. System confidence is mean centered. The variable of interest, the 3 way interaction using an n-size of 497: B = -.069, SE = .301, Wald = .053, p = .818. H was not supported." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
