Daniel M. Bartels, Oleg Urminsky

Journal of Consumer Research, 2015. DOI 10.1086/680670

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Bartels JournConsRes 2015 mrZ - Chandler - New Data Replication - 579, Chandler & Struhl (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. I found that though people were less willing to make a purchase when opportunity costs were salient (as reported by Frederick et al., 2019), this tendency was not moderated by how connected people felt to their future self. Thus, the focal hypothesis of Bartels and Urminsky (2015) was unsuccessful according to SCORE criteria. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
