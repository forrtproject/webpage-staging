Some Exploratory Findings on the Development of Musical Tastes

Morris B. Holbrook, Robert M. Schindler

Journal of Consumer Research, 1989. DOI 10.1086/209200

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • Is there a peak in popular music preference at a certain song-specific age? A replication of Holbrook & Schindler's 1989 study, Hemming (2013). Musicae Scientiae. Outcome recorded: mixed. a peak in musical preference across song-specific age could be confirmed, but it shifted from 23.47 years in the original study to 17.36 years in the replication.
