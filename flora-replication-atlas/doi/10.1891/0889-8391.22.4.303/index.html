Physical Exercise as Interoceptive Exposure Within a Brief Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment for Anxiety-Sensitive Women

Brigitte C. Sabourin, Sherry H. Stewart, Simon B. Sherry, Margo C. Watt, Jaye Wald, Valerie V. Grant

Journal of Cognitive Psychotherapy, 2008. DOI 10.1891/0889-8391.22.4.303

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Running as Interoceptive Exposure for Decreasing Anxiety Sensitivity: Replication and Extension, Sabourin et al. (2015). Cognitive Behaviour Therapy. Outcome recorded: successful. As predicted, high AS participants, but not low AS participants, experienced decreases in cognitive, affective, and somatic reactions to running over time. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
