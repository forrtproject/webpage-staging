Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Goeree Econometrica 2011 1574 - Fonseca - New Data Replication - 2w9k2, Parsons et al. (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. This replication attempt was extremely successful. This is perhaps not surprising, since if participants vote with their signal (i.e. guess jar 1 if they draw a red ball and guess jar 2 if they draw a blue ball), then the odds of selecting jar 1 when the majority of group membersdrew a red ball mechanically decline as the decision threshold increases from 5 to 7 to 9.
