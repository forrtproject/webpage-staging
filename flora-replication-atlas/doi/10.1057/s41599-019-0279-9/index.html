Jon Roozenbeek, Sander van der Linden

Palgrave Communications, 2019. DOI 10.1057/s41599-019-0279-9

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Good News about Bad News: Gamified Inoculation Boosts Confidence and Cognitive Immunity Against Fake News, Basol et al. (2020). Journal of Cognition. Outcome recorded: successful. We find that playing Bad News significantly improves people's ability to spot misinformation techniques compared to a gamified control group, and crucially, also increases people's level of confidence in their own judgments.
