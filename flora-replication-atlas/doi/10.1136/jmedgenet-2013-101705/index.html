Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • A replication study of a candidate locus for follicle-stimulating hormone levels and association analysis for semen quality traits in Japanese men, Sato et al. (2016). Journal of Human Genetics. Outcome recorded: successful. We successfully replicated, in Japanese men, the results of the previous GWAS for the rs2414095 SNP in Chinese men, and found that the rs2414095 SNP was related with sperm production.
