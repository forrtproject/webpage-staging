Markus Jokela, Marianna Virtanen, G. David Batty, Mika Kivimäki

JAMA Psychiatry, 2016. DOI 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2015.1977

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Using network analysis to examine links between individual depressive symptoms, inflammatory markers, and covariates, Fried et al. (2019). Psychological Medicine. Outcome recorded: mixed. In a conservative model with all 38 variables, symptoms and markers were unrelated. Following recent psychometric work, we re-estimated the full model without regularization: the depressive symptoms 'insomnia', 'hypersomnia', and 'aches and pain' showed unique positive relations to all inflammatory markers.
