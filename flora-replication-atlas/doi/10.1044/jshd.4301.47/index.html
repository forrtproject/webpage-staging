Replications

  • Is there an order of loss of sounds in speakers with Parkinson's disease?, Read et al. (2018). Clinical Linguistics & Phonetics. Outcome recorded: failed. Speech sound accuracy was strongly associated with intelligibility outcomes (p = < 0.01). Contrary to previous assertions, there was no evidence supporting the existence of a posterior to anterior order of 'loss' of oral speech sounds in people with PD, nor an interaction of anterior-posterior speech profile changes with Hoehn and Yahr stage.
