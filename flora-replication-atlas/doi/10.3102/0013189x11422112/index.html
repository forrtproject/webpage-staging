Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Robinson EduRes 2011 NrrW - Urbanska - Secondary Data Replication - 24812, Parsons et al. (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. In the replication dataset, straight-identified youth (85%) were more likely than LGB-identified youth (57%) to report not considering suicide, χ2(1) = 608.99, p = .00000000000000022 (two-tailed). The original paper did not report theeffect size so the effect size was not computed. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
