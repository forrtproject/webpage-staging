Replications

  • Shifting and zooming through space … or not: The role of attention in spatial compatibility tasks - A replication of Stoffer (1991), Baess et al. (2026). Psychological Research. Outcome recorded: mixed. With an adequately-powered sample, our study revealed concurrent global and local Simon effects – a result absent from Stoffer's original findings. Only the local Simon effect varied with precue type and simultaneous or sequential target presentation. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
