Replications

  • Spirituality and the Health of U. S. College Students: A Partial Replication and Extension, Miles & Naumann (2024). Journal of Organizational Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. In keeping with the findings of Nelms et al. (2007), we found that undergraduate students' self-reported higher level of spirituality was positively related to student self- reported level of physical health, emotional health, and exercise/sports.
