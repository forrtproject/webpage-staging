Romantic jealousy in early adulthood and in later life

Todd K. Shackelford, Martin Voracek, David P. Schmitt, David M. Buss, Viviana A. Weekes-Shackelford, Richard L. Michalski

Human Nature, 2004. DOI 10.1007/s12110-004-1010-z

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Sex Differences in Distress From Infidelity in Early Adulthood and in Later Life, IJzerman et al. (2014). Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. We conducted four high-powered replications (total N = 1,952) of this effect and found different results. A meta-analysis of original and replication studies finds the sex difference in younger samples (though with a smaller effect size), and no effect among older samples. Outcome read from the abstract.
