Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • Association of Three Genetic Loci with Molar Incisor Hypomineralization in Polish Children, Milona et al. (2024). Journal of Clinical Medicine. Outcome recorded: failed. Our replication study did not confirm highly significant associations between the single nucleotide polymorphisms rs2889956, rs4811117, and rs13058467 with molar incisor hypomineralization in Polish children.
