Sex-Dimorphic Face Shape Preference in Heterosexual and Homosexual Men and Women

Aaron N. Glassenberg, David R. Feinberg, Benedict C. Jones, Anthony C. Little, Lisa M. DeBruine

Archives of Sexual Behavior, 2009. DOI 10.1007/s10508-009-9559-6

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Sexual orientation predicts men's preferences for sexually dimorphic face-shape characteristics: A replication study, Shiramizu et al. (2020). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: successful. Here we directly replicated that study by comparing 623 gay and 3163 straight men's preferences for feminized versus masculinized versions of faces. Consistent with the adaptation-for-mate-choice hypothesis of straight men's femininity preferences, we found that straight men showed significantly stronger preferences for feminized female faces than did gay men.
