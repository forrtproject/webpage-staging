Effects of temperature on the mortality and growth of Hawaiian reef corals

P. L. Jokiel, S. L. Coles

Marine Biology, 1977. DOI 10.1007/bf00402312

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Evidence of acclimatization or adaptation in Hawaiian corals to higher ocean temperatures, Coles et al. (2018). PeerJ. Outcome recorded: successful. First whole colony mortality was also observed to occur sooner in 1970 than in 2017 in M. capitata (3 d vs. 15 d respectively), L. scutaria (3 d vs. 17 d), and in P. damicornis (3 d vs. 13 d).
