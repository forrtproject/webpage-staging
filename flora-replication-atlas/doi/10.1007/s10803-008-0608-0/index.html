Brief Report: Eye-Movement Patterns During an Embedded Figures Test in Children with ASD

Brandon Keehn, Laurie A. Brenner, Aurora I. Ramos, Alan J. Lincoln, Sandra P. Marshall, Ralph-Axel Müller

Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 2008. DOI 10.1007/s10803-008-0608-0

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Visual search strategies of children with and without autism spectrum disorders during an embedded figures task, Horlin et al. (2014). Research in Autism Spectrum Disorders. Outcome recorded: mixed. Children with ASD exhibited fewer fixations, and less time per fixation, on the target figure. Accuracy was similar between the two groups. Outcome read from the abstract.
