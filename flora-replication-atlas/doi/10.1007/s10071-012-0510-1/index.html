Deborah Custance, Jennifer Mayer

Animal Cognition, 2012. DOI 10.1007/s10071-012-0510-1

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • A shoulder to cry on: Heart rate variability and empathetic behavioral responses to crying and laughing in dogs, Meyers-Manor & Botten (2020). Canadian Journal of Experimental Psychology / Revue canadienne de psychologie expérimentale. Outcome recorded: successful. Like Custance and Mayer, dogs showed more behaviours directed toward the person crying, whether the owner or the stranger, than during baseline or laughing conditions. Outcome read from the abstract.
