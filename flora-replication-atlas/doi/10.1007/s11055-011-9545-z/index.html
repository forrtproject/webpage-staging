Prepulse Modification of the Acoustic Startle Reaction in Healthy Subjects and Patients with Schizophrenia

Z. I. Storozheva, A. V. Kirenskaya, I. E. Lazarev, V. Yu. Novototskii-Vlasov, D. V. Samylkin, G. A. Fastovtsov

Neuroscience and Behavioral Physiology, 2011. DOI 10.1007/s11055-011-9545-z

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • Startle Modification and P50 Gating in Schizophrenia Patients and Controls: Russian Population, Storozheva et al. (2016). The Spanish Journal of Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Obtained results demonstrated that both PPI and P50 could be regarded as potential schizophrenia biomarkers in Russian population. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
