Replications

  • Is "Constitutional Veneration" an Obstacle to Constitutional Amendment?, Dawes & Zink (2021). Journal of Experimental Political Science. Outcome recorded: successful. Our results build on Zink and Dawes's findings and show that, in addition to institutional factors, citizens' veneration of the Constitution can act as a psychological obstacle to constitutional amendment.
