Replications

  • Montez Demography 2014 3aPw - Cheng/Oyebanjo - Secondary Data Replication, Cheng et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. We found that respondents who face at least three adversities are statistically more likely in adulthood to transition from a state of no functional limitation to a state of difficulty with basic activities of daily living, when we control for the effect of childhood self-reported health only (model 1) Outcome read from OSF report.
