Implicit Science Stereotypes Mediate the Relationship between Gender and Academic Participation

Kristin A. Lane, Jin X. Goh, Erin Driver-Linn

Sex Roles, 2011. DOI 10.1007/s11199-011-0036-z

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • What Is the Implicit Gender-Science Stereotype? Exploring Correlations Between the Gender-Science IAT and Self-Report Measures, Zitelny et al. (2017). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: statistically successful but flawed. In a reanalysis of a previous study and a replication of another study, we found that evidence for the IAT's advantage over explicit stereotypes in predicting relevant outcomes disappeared when controlling for self-reported liking.
