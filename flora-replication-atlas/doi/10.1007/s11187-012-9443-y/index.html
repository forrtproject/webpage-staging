The higher returns to formal education for entrepreneurs versus employees

Mirjam Van Praag, Arjen van Witteloostuijn, Justin van der Sluis

Small Business Economics, 2013. DOI 10.1007/s11187-012-9443-y

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • The higher returns to formal education for entrepreneurs versus employees in Australia, Hessels et al. (2020). Journal of Business Venturing Insights. Outcome recorded: mixed. In accordance with Van Praag et al. (2013), we also find higher returns to education for entrepreneurs compared to employees. However, this finding mainly applies to the entrepreneurs without employees. Moreover, we do not find evidence for a mediating role of personal control in this relationship.
