Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Reproducibility of improvements in patient-reported functional ability following functional capacity evaluation, Schindl et al. (2022). BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. Outcome recorded: successful. A significant increase in patient-reported functional ability after FCE was found in the original study and the results could be reproduced in two subsequent cohorts.
