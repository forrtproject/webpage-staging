Dispositional use of emotion regulation strategies and resting-state cortico-limbic functional connectivity

Maria Picó-Pérez, Pino Alonso, Oren Contreras-Rodríguez, Ignacio Martínez-Zalacaín, Clara López-Solà, Susana Jiménez-Murcia, Antonio Verdejo-García, José M. Menchón, Carles Soriano-Mas

Brain Imaging and Behavior, 2018. DOI 10.1007/s11682-017-9762-3

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Resting State Cortico-Limbic Functional Connectivity and Dispositional Use of Emotion Regulation Strategies: A Replication and Extension Study, Dörfel et al. (2020). Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: failed. Opposed to prior findings, we were not able to replicate a correlation of dispositional ER strategy use with functional connectivity between amygdala and PFC regions (p > 0.05, FWE-corrected). The present preregistered study calls into question the reported association between individual differences in resting state cortico-limbic connectivity and dispositional use of ER strategies.
