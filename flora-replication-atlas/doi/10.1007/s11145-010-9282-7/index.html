Is preview benefit from word n + 2 a common effect in reading Chinese? Evidence from eye movements

Jinmian Yang, Keith Rayner, Nan Li, Suiping Wang

Reading and Writing, 2010. DOI 10.1007/s11145-010-9282-7

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • Word n + 2 preview effects in three-character Chinese idioms and phrases, Yu et al. (2016). Language, Cognition and Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: successful. Experiment 1 replicated prior findings, although additional analyses of word n + 1 and n + 2 as a single region revealed significant preview effects regardless of word n + 1 frequency.
