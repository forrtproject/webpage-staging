Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • A Multilab Replication of the Ego Depletion Effect, Dang et al. (2021). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: successful. Data from 12 labs across the globe (N = 1,775) revealed a small and significant ego depletion effect, d = 0.10. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
