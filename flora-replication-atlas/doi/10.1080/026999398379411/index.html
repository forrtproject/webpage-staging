Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 1999.

Replications

  • Some methodological issues in assessing attentional biases for threatening faces in anxiety: a replication study using a modified version of the probe detection task, Mogg & Bradley (1999). Behaviour Research and Therapy. Outcome recorded: successful. The probe position task produced similar results to those obtained from the probe classification task, so providing further evidence of an anxiety-related attentional bias for threatening faces.
