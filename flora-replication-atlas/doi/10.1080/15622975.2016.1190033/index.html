Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2025.

Replications

  • No indications of weight gain associated DNA methylation changes in patients with anorexia nervosa, Rajcsanyi et al. (2025). Scientific Reports. Outcome recorded: failed. no evidence was observed for hypomethylation at the investigated 15 CpG sites of the NR1H3 locus. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
