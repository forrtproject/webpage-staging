Order of difficulty of suggestions during hypnosis

Peter B. Field, Frederick J. Evans, Martin T. Orne

International Journal of Clinical and Experimental Hypnosis, 1965. DOI 10.1080/00207146508412939

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 1990.

Replications

  • Hypnosis, Suggestion Type, and Subjective Experience — The Order-Effects Hypothesis Revisited:A Brief Communication, Weekes & Lynn (1990). International Journal of Clinical and Experimental Hypnosis. Outcome recorded: failed. In a replication and extension of Field, Evans, and Orne's (1965) research, no support was found for the hypothesis that suggestion order is related to hypnotic responding. Confirming earlier findings, Ss were no more responsive to suggestions ordered from easy-to-difficult than they were to suggestions ordered from difficult-to-easy. Outcome read from the abstract.
