Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Instructor Clarity, Humor, Immediacy, and Student Learning: Replication and Extension, Violanti et al. (2018). Communication Studies. Outcome recorded: successful. This study replicated Richmond et al.'s (1987) study on nonverbal immediacy and cognitive learning (H1b), with statistically significant relationships (p < .01) between nonverbal immediacy and learning loss (r = -.49), nonverbal immediacy and perceived learning (r = .46) [...]. Correlations in the replication study revealed weaker relationships overall between nonverbal immediacy behaviors and both learning loss and perceived learning. Outcome read from p. 9, fulltext.
