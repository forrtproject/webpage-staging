Using the NAART to Estimate WRAT-4 Reading Grade Level in a Predominantly African American Sample

Glenn N. Jones, Daniel A. Proto, Mandi W. Musso, Alyse A. Barker, W. Drew Gouvier

Applied Neuropsychology: Adult, 2012. DOI 10.1080/09084282.2011.643966

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • A comparative study of the NAART and WRAT4 Word Reading subtest to estimate reading level, Campbell et al. (2021). Applied Neuropsychology: Adult. Outcome recorded: successful. This finding suggests that the original regression equation created by Jones et al. can be applied to the current sample to accurately predict reading level classification.
