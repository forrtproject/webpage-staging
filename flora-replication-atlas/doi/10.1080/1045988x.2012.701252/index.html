Academic Choice for Included Students with Emotional and Behavioral Disorders

Mandi Davis Skerbetz, Douglas E. Kostewicz

Preventing School Failure: Alternative Education for Children and Youth, 2013. DOI 10.1080/1045988x.2012.701252

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Empowering Teachers With Low-Intensity Strategies to Support Instruction: Implementing Across-Activity Choices During Third-Grade Reading Instruction, Ennis et al. (2019). Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. Outcome recorded: successful. Results of a withdrawal design indicated a functional relation between the introduction of instructional choice and increases in the academic engagement for the three students.
