Replications

  • Learning from Errors at Work: A Replication Study in Elder Care Nursing, Leicher et al. (2012). Vocations and Learning. Outcome recorded: successful. These results entirely cross-validate the exploratory findings of Bauer and Mulder (Learning in Health and Social Care 6:121-133, 2011) on hospital nurses' ESLA and show that they generalise to the domain of elder care nursing.
