Fast But Fleeting: Adaptive Motor Learning Processes Associated with Aging and Cognitive Decline

Kevin M. Trewartha, Angeles Garcia, Daniel M. Wolpert, J. Randall Flanagan

The Journal of Neuroscience, 2014. DOI 10.1523/jneurosci.1489-14.2014

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2025.

Replications

  • Not fleeting but lasting: limited influence of aging on implicit adaptative motor learning and its short-term retention, Hermans et al. (2025). Journal of Neurophysiology. Outcome recorded: failed. our conceptual replication failed to reproduce the result of Trewartha et al. (Trewartha KM, Garcia A, Wolpert DM, Flanagan JR. J Neurosci 34: 13411–13421, 2014) and found that the spontaneous recovery was also similar across groups. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
