Reward retroactively enhances memory consolidation for related items

Anuya Patil, Vishnu P. Murty, Joseph E. Dunsmoor, Elizabeth A. Phelps, Lila Davachi

Learning & Memory, 2017. DOI 10.1101/lm.042978.116

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • Reward conditioning may not have an effect on category-specific memory, Sukumaran et al. (2023). Scientific Reports. Outcome recorded: failed. None of our experiments showed consistent evidence for an effect of reward on category-specific memory enhancement, despite employing the same reward paradigm and incidental encoding protocol as in the original study.
