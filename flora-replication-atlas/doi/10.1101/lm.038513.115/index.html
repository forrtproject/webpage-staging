Joaquín M. Alfei, Roque I. Ferrer Monti, Victor A. Molina, Adrián M. Bueno, Gonzalo P. Urcelay

Learning & Memory, 2015. DOI 10.1101/lm.038513.115

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Limited replicability of drug-induced amnesia after contextual fear memory retrieval in rats, Schroyens et al. (2019). Neurobiology of Learning and Memory. Outcome recorded: failed. Most remarkably, our exact replication attempts that should have had sufficient power according to sample size calculations based on the original papers, did not provide robust evidence for amnesia either.
