Need for Cognition and Expectations as Determinants of Affective Experience

Andrew L. Geers, G. Daniel Lassiter

Basic and Applied Social Psychology, 2003. DOI 10.1207/s15324834basp2504_5

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • No Evidence for the Affective Expectation Model: Replication of Geers and Lassiter (2003), Röseler et al. (2024). Outcome recorded: failed. None of the original effects could be replicated, that is, affective experiences did not differ between the groups and were not moderated by need for cognition. Outcome read from the abstract.
