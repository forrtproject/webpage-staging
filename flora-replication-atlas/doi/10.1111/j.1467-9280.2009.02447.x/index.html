Asymmetrical Body Perception

Sally A. Linkenauger, Jessica K. Witt, Jonathan Z. Bakdash, Jeanine K. Stefanucci, Dennis R. Proffitt

Psychological Science, 2009. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02447.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • It's out of my hands! Grasping capacity may not influence perceived object size, Collier & Lawson (2017). Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated the finding that right handers overestimate the size and grasping capacity of their right hand relative to their left hand. However, when estimates of object size and grasping capacity were made in separate tasks, objects grasped in the right hand were not underestimated relative to those grasped in the left hand.   • SCORE new data replication of Linkenauger et al. (2009), Brady et al. (2026). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. As shown in Figure 1, we found no evidence of the interaction reported in the original paper, which had reported that right-handed participants and only right-handed participants reported their left arm as shorter than it was. Instead, we largely found similar results for left and right arms of left-handed and right-handed participants, with both left-handed and right-handed individuals reliably underestimating both of their arm lengths to similar extent. Reproductions

  • Reproduction (with author data): Linkenauger et al. (2009, Psychological Science), Parsons et al. (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: computational issues, not checked. Computational: "Reproduction outcome: Based on these criteria, the claim did not reproduce" (stated for all 3 claims); primary criteria—sample size, focal test statistic (F=4.74, t=2.38), and effect size—all matched precisely, but secondary p-rep criteria showed mismatches (approximate or non-reproduction) in each claim, e.g. Table 3: p-rep (right arm) original 0.50 vs reproduced 0.72 flagged "non-reproduction".
