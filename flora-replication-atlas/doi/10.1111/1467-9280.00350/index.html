Barbara Mellers, Ralph Hertwig, Daniel Kahneman

Psychological Science, 2001. DOI 10.1111/1467-9280.00350

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Frequency estimation and semantic ambiguity do not eliminate ‎conjunction bias, when it ‎occurs: Replication and extension of ‎Mellers, Hertwig, and Kahneman (2001)‎, Chandrashekar et al. (2021). Meta-Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. Linda profile findings were in sup- port of conjunction effect and consistent with Tversky and Kahneman's (1983) arguments for a representative heuristic. We found no support for semantic ambiguity. Findings for James profile were a likely failed replication, with no conjunction effect. View paper
