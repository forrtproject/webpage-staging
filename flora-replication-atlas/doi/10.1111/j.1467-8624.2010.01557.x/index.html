Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • A Large-Scale Replication of the Effectiveness of the KiVa Antibullying Program: a Randomized Controlled Trial in the Netherlands, Huitsing et al. (2020). Prevention Science. Outcome recorded: successful. These longitudinal models showed that self-reported victimization and bullying reduced more strongly in KiVa-schools compared with control schools.
