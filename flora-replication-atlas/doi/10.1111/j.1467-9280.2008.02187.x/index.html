Adaptive Responses to Social Exclusion

Michael J. Bernstein, Steven G. Young, Christina M. Brown, Donald F. Sacco, Heather M. Claypool

Psychological Science, 2008. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02187.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Does Social Exclusion Improve Detection of Real and Fake Smiles? A Replication Study, Schindler & Trede (2021). Frontiers in Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. In line with the original study, results showed that participants in the exclusion condition performed better than in the control condition. However, the performance did not differ between the exclusion and inclusion condition - although the pattern was in the predicted direction. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
