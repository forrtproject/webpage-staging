Explicit and Implicit Lexical Knowledge: Acquisition of Collocations Under Different Input Conditions

Suhad Sonbul, Norbert Schmitt

Language Learning, 2012. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9922.2012.00730.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • The Development of Implicit and Explicit Knowledge of Collocations: A Conceptual Replication and Extension of Sonbul and Schmitt (2013), Toomer & Elgort (2019). Language Learning. Outcome recorded: mixed. These results replicated Sonbul and Schmitt's findings for explicit knowledge and extended their findings for implicit knowledge. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • The effects of different learning conditions on the development of collocational knowledge in a second language, Toomer (2019). Outcome recorded: successful. The results of the immediate cued-recall and form-recognition post-tests corroborate Sonbul and Schmitt's findings: multiple encounters with the collocations produced substantial declarative collocational knowledge, and more declarative knowledge was produced through exposure to typographically-enhanced collocations (with and without glosses) than to typographically-unenhanced collocations. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
