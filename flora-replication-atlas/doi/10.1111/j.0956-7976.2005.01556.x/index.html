Linguistic Constraints on Statistical Computations

Luca L. Bonatti, Marcela Peña, Marina Nespor, Jacques Mehler

Psychological Science, 2005. DOI 10.1111/j.0956-7976.2005.01556.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Replication of Bonatti, Peña, Nespor, & Mehler (2005) Exp. 2a, Craparotta et al. (2022). Outcome recorded: failed. In both cases, we found a small but reliable effect, with subjects successfully tracking vowel transition probabilities. This is in contrast to the original paper, which found no such effect. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Replication of Bonatti, Peña, Nespor, & Mehler (2005) Experiment 1, Feinberg et al. (2022). PsyArXiv. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated the study with both French-speaking subjects (as in the original) and English-speaking subjects. In both cases, we found a small but reliable effect, with subjects successfully tracking consonant transition probabilities. This replicates the findings of the original study. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
