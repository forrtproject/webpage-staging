Effects of Stimulus Characteristics and Background Music on Foreign Language Vocabulary Learning and Forgetting

Annette M. B. de Groot

Language Learning, 2006. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9922.2006.00374.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • EEG Beta Power but Not Background Music Predicts the Recall Scores in a Foreign-Vocabulary Learning Task, Küssner et al. (2016). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: mixed. A combined analysis of both experiments suggests that beta power predicts the performance in the word recall task, but that there is no effect of background music on foreign vocabulary learning. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
