Task difficulty effects on cardiac activity

Michael Richter, Antonia Friedrich, Guido H. E. Gendolla

Psychophysiology, 2008. DOI 10.1111/j.1469-8986.2008.00688.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2026.

Replications

  • A well-powered test of task difficulty effects on cardiovascular indicators of mental effort, Brinkmann et al. (2026). International Journal of Psychophysiology. Outcome recorded: successful. As expected, responses of PEP-and also cardiac output (CO), SBP, DBP, and HR-were stronger during the difficult compared to the easy task, thereby conceptually replicating and extending past research.
