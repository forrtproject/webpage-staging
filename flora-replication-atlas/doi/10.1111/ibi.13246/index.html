Preen oil composition of Pied Flycatchers is similar between partners but differs between sexes and breeding stages

Marc Gilles, Rienk W. Fokkema, Peter Korsten, Barbara A. Caspers, Tim Schmoll

Ibis, 2023. DOI 10.1111/ibi.13246

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • Chemical signatures in the preen oil of pied flycatchers: testing reproducibility and exploring ontogeny, Jeanjean et al. (2024). Journal of Avian Biology. Outcome recorded: mixed. Unlike the original study, we found no difference in chemical profiles between sexes (nestling‐rearing), casting doubt on the proposed role of preen oil as a sex semiochemical in this species.
