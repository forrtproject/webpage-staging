God Is Watching You

Azim F. Shariff, Ara Norenzayan

Psychological Science, 2007. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.01983.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • The effects of implicit religious primes on dictator game allocations: A preregistered replication experiment, Gomes & McCullough (2015). Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. Outcome recorded: failed. We did not find a statistically significant effect for religious priming. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Does religious priming increase the prosocial behaviour of a Japanese sample in an anonymous economic game?, Miyatake & Higuchi (2017). Asian Journal of Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. This study aimed to conduct a replication of Shariff and Norenzayan (2007) using a Japanese college student sample to examine whether the results of the original study could be applicable to Japanese people. The results showed that this study failed to replicate the main findings of the original study. Outcome read from the full text. View paper
