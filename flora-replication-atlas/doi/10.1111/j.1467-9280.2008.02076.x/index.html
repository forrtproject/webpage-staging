Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: failed. This replication found results qualitatively similar to those of Ranganath & Nosek (2008). However, it did not replicate their findings according to our a priori criterion requiring both a significant difference between ratings of the original and the new individuals on the explicit measure, and a lack of difference on the implicit measure.
