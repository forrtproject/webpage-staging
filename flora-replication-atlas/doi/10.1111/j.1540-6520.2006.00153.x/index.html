Corporate Entrepreneurship in Family Firms: A Family Perspective

Franz W. Kellermanns, Kimberly A. Eddleston

Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice, 2006. DOI 10.1111/j.1540-6520.2006.00153.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Entrepreneurial behavior in family firms: A replication study, Weismeier-Sammer (2011). Journal of Family Business Strategy. Outcome recorded: successful. Compared to the original study, the replication's results coincide in most parts. However, regarding the relation between strategic planning efforts and entrepreneurial behavior, the replication provides new insights into the entrepreneurial behavior of family firms by stressing the importance of considering organizational size in family-firm research.
