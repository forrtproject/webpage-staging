Replications

  • Registered Report: A Replication Examining Occupational Experience and Performance on the Water-Level Task, Tenney et al. (2026). Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. We failed to replicate Hecht and Proffitt's results, finding that their study had less than 33% power to detect the small, nonsignificant difference that we observed between groups.
