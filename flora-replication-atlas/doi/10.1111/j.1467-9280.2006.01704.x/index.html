Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Results Report: Replication of Epley and Gilovich (2006, Study 2a), Kroworsch et al. (2021). OSF Registries. Outcome recorded: failed. Thus, we deem the replication an informative failure: there is evidence against the hypothesis that forewarning moderates adjustment from self-generated anchors. Outcome read from p. 39. View paper
  • Need for Cognition, Cognitive Load, and Forewarning do not Moderate Anchoring Effects. A Replication Study of Epley & Gilovich (Journal of Behavioral Decision Making, 2005; Psychological Science, 2006), Röseler et al. (2024). Journal of Comments and Replications in Economics. Outcome recorded: failed. We conducted three replications (N total = 657) of seminal studies on the distinction between self-generated and experimenter-provided anchoring effects where we investigated the moderators need for cognition, cognitive load, and forewarning. We found no evidence that either type of anchoring is moderated by any of the moderators.
