Eighteen‐ and 24‐month‐old infants correct others in anticipation of action mistakes

Birgit Knudsen, Ulf Liszkowski

Developmental Science, 2011. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-7687.2011.01098.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Replications of implicit theory of mind tasks with varying representational demands, Powell et al. (2018). Cognitive Development. Outcome recorded: successful. These results replicate Knudsen and Liszkowski's (2012) findings with 24-month-old children and partially extend them to 3-year-old children, suggesting that an age group population of children well known to fail explicit false belief tasks is nonetheless motivated by an experimenter's lack of awareness to engage in more helpful, informing behaviors.
