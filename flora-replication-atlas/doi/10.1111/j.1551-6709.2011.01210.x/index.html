Reflection and Reasoning in Moral Judgment

Joseph M. Paxton, Leo Ungar, Joshua D. Greene

Cognitive Science, 2011. DOI 10.1111/j.1551-6709.2011.01210.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Estimating the Reproducibility of Experimental Philosophy, Cova et al. (2018). Review of Philosophy and Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. Thus, the two original effects failed to replicate. Outcome read from osf.io. View paper
  • Eleven years of student replication projects provide evidence on the correlates of replicability in psychology, Boyce et al. (2023). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. The rescue did not reproduce the main result found in the original: participants who were shown CRT questions before moral dilemmas did not deliver stronger utilitarian responses. Outcome read from discussion of report. View paper
