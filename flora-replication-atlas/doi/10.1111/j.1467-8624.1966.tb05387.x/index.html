Child Development, 1966. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-8624.1966.tb05387.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Multilab Direct Replication of Flavell, Beach, and Chinsky (1966): Spontaneous Verbal Rehearsal in a Memory Task as a Function of Age, Elliott et al. (2021).
