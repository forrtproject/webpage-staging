Induction of LTP in the human auditory cortex by sensory stimulation

W. C. Clapp, I. J. Kirk, J. P. Hamm, D. Shepherd, T. J. Teyler

European Journal of Neuroscience, 2005. DOI 10.1111/j.1460-9568.2005.04293.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Do visual and auditory stimulus‐specific response modulation reflect different mechanisms of neocortical plasticity?, Rygvold et al. (2021). European Journal of Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: failed. The AEPs did not fully replicate previous work, as there were no significant amplitude change of the N1 component from baseline to post-stimulation blocks, an effect that was expected given that the auditory paradigm in the present study was modelled after the initial report on the phenomenon (Clapp, Kirk, et al., 2005).
