Replications

  • Measuring the crowd within again: a pre-registered replication study, Steegen et al. (2014). Frontiers in Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. We successfully replicated the crowd within effect, both when the second guess was made immediately after the first guess, as well as when it was made 3 weeks later.
