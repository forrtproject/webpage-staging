Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • The Godden and Baddeley (1975) experiment on context-dependent memory on land and underwater: a replication, Murre (2021). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to the original study, we did not find that recall in the same context where the words had been learned was better than recall in the other context. Outcome read from the abstract.
