Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Unlike in Nurmsoo and Bloom (2008), the confirmatory analysis for the current replication attempt did not reveal above-chance performance by older children on "where" trials. Thus, it is clear that we failed to replicate the original study's finding. Outcome read from p. 5. View paper
