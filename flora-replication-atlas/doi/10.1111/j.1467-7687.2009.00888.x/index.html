Online usage of theory of mind continues to develop in late adolescence

Iroise Dumontheil, Ian A. Apperly, Sarah-Jayne Blakemore

Developmental Science, 2010. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-7687.2009.00888.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • Development of online use of theory of mind during adolescence: An eye-tracking study, Symeonidou et al. (2016). Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated previous findings, namely that in the director-present condition, but not in the control condition, children and adolescents made more errors than adults, suggesting that theory of mind use improves between adolescence and adulthood.
