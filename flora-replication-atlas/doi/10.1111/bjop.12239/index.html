Working memory moderates the effect of the integrative process of implicit and explicit autonomous motivation on academic achievement

Alexandre Gareau, Patrick Gaudreau

British Journal of Psychology, 2017. DOI 10.1111/bjop.12239

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Past academic achievement contributes to university students' autonomous motivation (AM) which is later moderated by implicit motivation and working memory: A Bayesian replication of the explicit-implicit model of AM, Gareau et al. (2019). Learning and Individual Differences. Outcome recorded: successful. The Bayesian model replicated the results of Gareau and Gaudreau (2017), thus strengthening the position of adopting a dual-process perspective of AM. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
