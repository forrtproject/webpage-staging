Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: successful. The central claim of their paper – that anchor precision influences the degree of adjustment was supported. Additionally, these data replicated the main effect of motivation observed in their study. There was no evidence of the reported interaction. Outcome read from p. 3. View paper
