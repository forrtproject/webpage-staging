Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. We were able to replicate the significant main effect of ISI which showed that performance worsened as the interval between a chord and tone increased. Next, we also replicated the non-significant main effect of N, showing that N did not significantly affect performance. Finally, in contrast with the original study, we found that the effect of ISI on performance did not differ between chords with small or large N.
