Action Anticipation Through Attribution of False Belief by 2-Year

V. Southgate, A. Senju, G. Csibra

Psychological Science, 2007. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.01944.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 3 failed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • A two-lab direct replication attempt of Southgate, Senju and Csibra (2007), Kampis et al. (2021). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. We found no evidence for action anticipation: only roughly half of the infants looked to the location of an agent's impending action when action prediction did not require taking into account the agent's beliefs Outcome read from the abstract.
