Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: failed. While we were clearly able to replicate the basic ERN and EPS results, the main result ofthe original study was the correlation between the two. The correlation in our replication trended in the same direction as that of the original study, but did not reach the p < .05 threshold for significance. For this reason, we consider this to be a failure to replicate but with extremely similar results. Outcome read from p. 5 of replication report. View paper
