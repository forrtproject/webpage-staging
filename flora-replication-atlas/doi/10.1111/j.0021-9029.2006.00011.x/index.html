  • Structure of food attitudes: Replication of Aikman, Crites, and Fabrigar (2006), Aikman & Crites (2007). Appetite. Outcome recorded: successful. The goal of the present study was to replicate the food attitude factor structure identified in Aikman et al. (2006) using a different population. Overall, the results of these analyses are consistent with the findings of Aikman et al. (2006). Importantly, the five-factor food attitude model previously identified was again shown to have acceptable fit across most foods.
