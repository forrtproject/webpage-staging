SUZANNE OWEN, DINAH GOULD

Journal of Clinical Nursing, 1997. DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2702.1997.tb00307.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 1999.

Replications

  • Patients' experiences of having an underwater seal chest drain: a replication study, Fox et al. (1999). Journal of Clinical Nursing. Outcome recorded: successful. This small-scale study was replicated to produce broadly similar findings in a second hospital 2 years later. Despite some differences in the approach to management it was apparent that patients were still not well prepared pre-operatively and there was scope for improving pain control.
