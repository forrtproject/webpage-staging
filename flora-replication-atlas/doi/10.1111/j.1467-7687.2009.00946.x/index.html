Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Actions do not speak louder than words in an interactive false belief task, Wenzel et al. (2020). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. the original results could not be replicated, and there was no evidence for FB understanding measured by the Sefo task in any age group except for adults.
