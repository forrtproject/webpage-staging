Maternal Sensitivity and Patterns of Infant-Mother Attachment

Philip B. Smith, David R. Pederson

Child Development, 1988. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-8624.1988.tb03262.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 1999.

Replications

  • Parental responsiveness and infant-parent attachment: a replication study with fathers and mothers, Notaro & Volling (1999). Infant Behavior and Development. Outcome recorded: failed. Results revealed that for both mothers and fathers, parental behavior in the No-toy situation neither differed according to attachment classification, nor could accurately predict the quality of mother-infant or father-infant attachment relationships.
