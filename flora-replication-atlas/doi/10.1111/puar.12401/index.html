Representative Bureaucracy and the Willingness to Coproduce: An Experimental Study

Norma M. Riccucci, Gregg G. Van Ryzin, Huafang Li

Public Administration Review, 2015. DOI 10.1111/puar.12401

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • A replication of "Representative bureaucracy and the willingness to coproduce", Sievert (2021). Public Administration. Outcome recorded: failed. The results do not confirm a positive effect of symbolic gender representation on willingness to co‐produce. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Representative bureaucracy and its symbolic effect on citizens: a conceptual replication, Van Ryzin et al. (2017). Public Management Review. Outcome recorded: failed. However, despite using a similar sample and experimental paradigm, we find no effects. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
