Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2005.

Replications

  • A Comparison of Therapy Alone Versus Therapy and Medication in a Community Clinic, Brown et al. (2005). Journal of Cognitive Psychotherapy. Outcome recorded: failed. The present study attempted to replicate those findings in an outpatient community clinic. Results showed that clients who took medications fared no better than clients who did not.
