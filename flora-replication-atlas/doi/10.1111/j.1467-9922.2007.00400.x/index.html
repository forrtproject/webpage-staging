Replications

  • THE EFFECTS OF INPUT ENHANCEMENT ON GRAMMAR LEARNING AND COMPREHENSION, Winke (2013). Studies in Second Language Acquisition. Outcome recorded: mixed. In contrast to Lee's results, I found enhancement did not significantly increase form correction gain scores, nor did enhancement significantly detract from comprehension.
