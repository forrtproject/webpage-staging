The Representation of Simple Ensemble Visual Features Outside the Focus of Attention

George A. Alvarez, Aude Oliva

Psychological Science, 2008. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02098.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. In this replication we found that both multiple and single distractors can be localized better than chance. That means we could replicate the result for centroid localization, but not for single distractor localization. Finally, the crucial aim of this experiment was to withdraw attention from distractors by spatially separating targets and distractors: "targets remained spatially separate from the distractors for the entirety of each trial" (Alvarez & Oliva, 2008, p. 396)… Outcome read from the discussion.
