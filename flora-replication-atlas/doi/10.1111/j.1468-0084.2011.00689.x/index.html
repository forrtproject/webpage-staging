Gender Differences in Entrepreneurial Propensity*

Philipp Koellinger, Maria Minniti, Christian Schade

Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics, 2011. DOI 10.1111/j.1468-0084.2011.00689.x

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Perceptual factors explaining the gender gap in entrepreneurial propensity: A replication and extension, Abbasianchavari & Block (2022). Journal of Business Venturing Insights. Outcome recorded: mixed. Our findings show that gender differences in entrepreneurial propensity still exist (although the effect size has been reduced substantially) and can be attributed to differences in perceptions of entrepreneurial skills. Surprisingly, the negative effect attributed to female respondents becomes positive after taking into account differences in skill perception, meaning that women have a higher entrepreneurial propensity than men.
