Replications

  • The Effect of Speaker Proficiency on Intelligibility, Comprehensibility, and Accentedness in L2 Spanish: A Conceptual Replication and Extension of Munro and Derwing (1995a), Huensch & Nagle (2021). Language Learning. Outcome recorded: successful. The current study's conceptual replication of Munro and Derwing (1995a) has broadened the generalizability of the initial findings, particularly with respect to intelligibility, in its focus on instructed L2 Spanish learners. The results generally patterned similarly to the initial studies, indicating evidence of generalizability particularly with regard to the robustness of partial independence among speech dimensions. Outcome read from the conclusion. View paper
