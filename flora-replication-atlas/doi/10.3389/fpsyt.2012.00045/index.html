Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation of the Frontal Eye Fields during Pro- and Antisaccade Tasks

Ryota Kanai, Neil Muggleton, Vincent Walsh

Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2012. DOI 10.3389/fpsyt.2012.00045

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • No Evidence That Frontal Eye Field tDCS Affects Latency or Accuracy of Prosaccades, Reteig et al. (2018). Frontiers in Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: failed. We did not find the same effect: neither anodal nor cathodal tDCS influenced the latency of lateral saccades. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Transcranial direct current stimulation of the right frontal eye field to affect saccade execution, Reteig et al. (2017). Journal of Vision. Outcome recorded: failed. Our initial results show that we are unable to reproduce the effect found by Kanai et al.: neither form of tDCS affected the median latency of contra- or ipsilateral saccades. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
