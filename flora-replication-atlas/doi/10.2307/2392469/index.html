Richard L. Daft, Norman B. Macintosh

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 1997.

Replications

  • A replication of the amount and equivocality of information processing in organizational work units, Lind (1997). Work Study. Outcome recorded: mixed. identifies some contradictions, but confirms Daft and Macintosh's unhypothesized findings and suggests that within an organizational context the amount of information processed by work units serves as an uncertainty reducing mechanism.
