Sex Differences: A Study of the Eye of the Beholder

John Condry, Sandra Condry

Child Development, 1976. DOI 10.2307/1128199

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2010.

Replications

  • Gender Label and Perceived Infant Emotionality: A Partial Replication of a Classic Study, Steuer et al. (2010). Psychological Reports. Outcome recorded: mixed. Participants in the present partial replication of the Condrys' study did not rate the infant differently based on the infant's gender label Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Sex Role Type as a Predictor of Gender Stereotype Use in the Evaluation of Others, Miller (2019). San Jose State University Scholar Works (San Jose State University). Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to the Condry findings, this study found that participant perception of why a baby was crying was not consistent with the gender label given (consistent, being angry for the boy label and fearful for the girl label). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • GENDER LABEL AND PERCEIVED INFANT EMOTIONALITY: A PARTIAL REPLICATION OF A CLASSIC STUDY, STEUER et al. (2010). Psychological Reports. Outcome recorded: failed. did not rate the infant differently based on the infant's gender label Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
