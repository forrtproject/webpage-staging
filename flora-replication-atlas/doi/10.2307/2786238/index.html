Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 1976.

Replications

  • Dimes and Helping: The Other Side of the Coin, Weyant & Clark (1976). Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to their findings, those subjects who found a dime did not mail an apparently lost letter more often than did those who did not find a dime. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
