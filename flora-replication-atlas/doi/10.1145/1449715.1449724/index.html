Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • OctoPocus in VR: Using a Dynamic Guide for 3D Mid-Air Gestures in Virtual Reality, Fennedy et al. (2021). IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics. Outcome recorded: mixed. Like our study, Bau and Mackay found OctoPocus significantly more accurate than a crib-sheet baseline, suggesting that OctoPocus is better at training people to perform more precise gesture movements in 2D and 3D. However, Bau and Mackay also found overall trial time for OctoPocus lower than crib-sheet, while we observed no overall difference in trial time, there is a trade-off between reaction time and input time.
