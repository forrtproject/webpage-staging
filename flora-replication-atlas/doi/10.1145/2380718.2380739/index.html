Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • The collective intelligence of random small crowds: A partial replication of Kosinski et al. (2012), Vercammen et al. (2019). Judgment and Decision Making. Outcome recorded: successful. We found that – on average – a group of six MTurkers has a collective IQ one standard deviation above the mean for the general population, thus demonstrating a "wisdom of the crowd" effect.
