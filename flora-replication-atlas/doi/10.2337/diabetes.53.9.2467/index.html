Genetic Variation in BEACON Influences Quantitative Variation in Metabolic Syndrome–Related Phenotypes

Jeremy B. Jowett, Kate S. Elliott, Joanne E. Curran, Nicola Hunt, Ken R. Walder, Greg R. Collier, Paul Z. Zimmet, John Blangero

Diabetes, 2004. DOI 10.2337/diabetes.53.9.2467

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2006.

Replications

  • Association of Genetic Variation Within UBL5 with Phenotypes of Metabolic Syndrome, Bozaoglu et al. (2006). Human Biology. Outcome recorded: successful. we found significant associations between UBL5 genetic variants and waist-to-hip ratio (p = 0.027), and the circulating concentrations of insulin (p = 0.018) and total cholesterol (p = 0.023) in fasted individuals.
