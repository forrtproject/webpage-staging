Saccade suppression depends on context

Eckart Zimmermann

eLife, 2020. DOI 10.7554/elife.49700

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Saccade suppression of displacements, but not of contrast, depends on context, Zimmermann & Lange (2022). Journal of Vision. Outcome recorded: successful. Here, we aimed to test whether saccade suppression of contrast is similarly modulated by context. To this end, we projected stimuli on a homogeneously white wall such that we could establish a ganzfeld-like environment that, depending on the experimental session, did or did not contain any visible contrast stimuli. We first successfully replicated the context sensitivity of saccade suppression of displacements. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
