The Dark Side of a Smiley

Ella Glikson, Arik Cheshin, Gerben A. van Kleef

Social Psychological and Personality Science, 2017. DOI 10.1177/1948550617720269

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • The Dark Versus Bright Side of a Smiley: A Preregistered Replication of Experiment 3 in Glikson et al. (2018) "The Dark Side of a Smiley", Lai & Mayiwar (2023). Collabra: Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. The authors of the original study reported support for their proposition that [...] the use of smileys may backfire and produce less favorable perceptions of competence in a formal work-related setting, yet more favorable perceptions of warmth in an informal work-related setting. Our results, in contrast, indicated that smileys produce a negative effect on perceptions of competence and a positive effect on perceptions of warmth, regardless of the level of formality.
