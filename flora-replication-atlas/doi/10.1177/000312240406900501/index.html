Sociology of Education. Outcome recorded: mixed. When we switch to the new test scores, however, we find that Conclusion 1 no longer holds. Inequality in test scores does not grow; it slightly shrinks between the start of kindergarten and the end of first or second grade. This is true for total variance in test scores, for the SES gap, and for most racial and ethnic gaps.
