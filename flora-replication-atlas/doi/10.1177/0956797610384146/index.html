Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Stereotypes Concerns and Discreet Existence of Differences between Men and Women in Risk-Aversion – a Replication Study, Adamus et al. (2021). Studia Psychologica. Outcome recorded: failed. In general, the study found no differences between men and women in risk aversion and did not replicate the original effect of stereotype threat on risk aversion. Outcome read from the abstract.
