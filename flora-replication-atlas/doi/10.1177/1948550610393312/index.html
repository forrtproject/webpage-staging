"Why Hast Thou Forsaken Me?" The Effect of Thinking About Being Ostracized by God on Well-Being and Prosocial Behavior

Ilja van Beest, Kipling D. Williams

Social Psychological and Personality Science, 2011. DOI 10.1177/1948550610393312

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • The Effect Of Thinking About Being Excluded By God On Well-Being: A Replication And Extension, George (2019). Outcome recorded: successful. The confirmatory hypothesis of the main effect of exclusion on well-being was supported. Christians who thought about being abandoned by their God (exclusion condition) had lowered well-being compared to Christians who thought about their God always including them (inclusion condition) or their God creating the Earth (control condition).
