Read-Out of Emotional Information From Iconic Memory

Christof Kuhbandner, Bernhard Spitzer, Reinhard Pekrun

Psychological Science, 2011. DOI 10.1177/0956797611406445

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Revisiting the prioritization of emotional information in iconic memory, Kattner & Clausen (2020). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: successful. Both experiments demonstrated prioritized selection of emotional targets from iconic memory, with higher verbal recall and visual recognition accuracy for negative and positive targets compared to neutral targets.
