Racial Disparities in Autism Identification in the United States During 2014

Jason Travers, Michael Krezmien

Exceptional Children, 2018. DOI 10.1177/0014402918771337

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Travers ExChildren 2018 zqwm - Sisco/Méndez-Chacón - Secondary Data Replication, Méndez-Chacón et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. The result suggests that, compared to California, Ohio is significantly less likely to identify students with autism. Ohio's odds ratio of autism identification is 0.91, with a 95% confidence interval equal to [0.90, 0.93], i.e., it lies entirely below 1.0. The odds ratio is statistically significant at the 5% level(p = 0.000). Thus, this replication of the claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
