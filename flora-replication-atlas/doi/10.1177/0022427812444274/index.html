The Causal Impact of Exposure to Deviant Peers

Ray Paternoster, Jean Marie McGloin, Holly Nguyen, Kyle J. Thomas

Journal of Research in Crime and Delinquency, 2013. DOI 10.1177/0022427812444274

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • An experimental investigation of the influence of deviant peers on own deviancy: A replication study, Mercer et al. (2018). Journal of Experimental Criminology. Outcome recorded: successful. Although not identical in magnitude, our results echo those found by Paternoster et al. (2013): Even a brief exposure to a previously unknown deviant peer increases the amount of deviant behavior in young adults. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
