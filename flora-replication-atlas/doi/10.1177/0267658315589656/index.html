Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Eye gaze and L2 speakers' responses to recasts: A systematic replication study of McDonough, Crowther, Kielstra and Trofimovich (2015), McDonough et al. (2018). Language Teaching. Outcome recorded: mixed. The positive relationship between mutual gaze and targetlike response confirms the findings of the initial study (McDonough et al. 2015), and extends the role of mutual gaze to recasts of different lengths in response to more varied error types. However, the negative relationship between L2 speaker eye gaze and targetlike responses is contrary to the earlier finding of a positive relationship.
