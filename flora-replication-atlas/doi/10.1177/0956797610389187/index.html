Replications

  • Do we see it or not? Sensory attenuation in the visual domain, Schwarz et al. (2018). Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to previous assumptions and despite high statistical power, however, the authors found no evidence for sensory attenuation of learned visual action effects. Outcome read from the abstract.
