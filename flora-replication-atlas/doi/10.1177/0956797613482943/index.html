Replications

  • From self to ingroup reclaiming of homophobic epithets: A replication and extension of Galinsky et al.'s (2013) model of reappropriation, Bianchi et al. (2024). Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Our results replicate the findings of Galinsky et al.'s (2013) on self-labelling, and they are the first to suggest that ingroup-labelling is also effective at counteracting stigmatising labels.
