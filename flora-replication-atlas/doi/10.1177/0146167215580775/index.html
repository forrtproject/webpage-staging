Choking Under Social Pressure

Megan L. Knowles, Gale M. Lucas, Roy F. Baumeister, Wendi L. Gardner

Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 2015. DOI 10.1177/0146167215580775

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Loneliness and social monitoring: A conceptual replication of Knowles et al, Floyd & Woo (2020). Personal Relationships. Outcome recorded: failed. This study conceptually replicates the central hypothesis of Knowles et al. using a sample of adults that is substantially larger and more diverse demographically and geographically, and using a true control group in addition to the comparison group. Results revealed a significant main effect of loneliness on social monitoring ability but did not replicate the choking under pressure phenomenon. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
