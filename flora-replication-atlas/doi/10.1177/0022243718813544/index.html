Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Data Replicada #1: Do Elevated Viewpoints Increase Risk Taking?, Simmons & Nelson (2019). Data Colada. Outcome recorded: failed. In sum, using three times the sample size as the original study, we find no evidence that being exposed to an elevated vertical position increases risk taking, at least when risk is operationalized as the likelihood of purchasing new products. Outcome read from Conclusion section.
