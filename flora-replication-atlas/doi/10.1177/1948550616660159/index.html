  • Do Animals' Minds Matter Less, When Meat Gets Personal? Replications of Piazza and Loughnan (2016) in China, Tian et al. (2021). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. We replicated the original three studies in China. Study 1 finds that the intelligence information about a fictional animal does not affect moral standing judgment or hunting decisions, inconsistent with the original study.
