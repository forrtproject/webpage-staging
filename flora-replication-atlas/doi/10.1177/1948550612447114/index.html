Replications

  • Direct and Conceptual Replications of Eskine (2013), Moery & Calin-Jageman (2016). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: failed. we found that organic food exposure has little to no effect on moral judgments ( d = 0.06, 95% confidence interval [CI] [−0.14, 0.26], N = 377) and prosocial behavior ( d = 0.03, 95% CI [−0.17, 0.23], N = 377).
