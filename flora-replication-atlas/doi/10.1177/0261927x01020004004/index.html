Name Your Favorite Musician

Dagmar Stahlberg, Sabine Sczesny, Friederike Braun

Journal of Language and Social Psychology, 2001. DOI 10.1177/0261927x01020004004

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Effects of the Generic Masculine and Its Alternatives in Germanophone Countries: A Multi-Lab Replication and Extension of Stahlberg, Sczesny, and Braun (2001), Brohmer et al. (2024). International Review of Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Our data from twelve labs and N = 2,697 participants replicated the original effect: when prompted with gender-inclusive forms participants mentioned more women than when the generic masculine and the control form were used. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Ladies First or Ladies Last: Do Masculine Generics Evoke a Reduced and Later Retrieval of Female Exemplars?, Keith et al. (2022). Collabra: Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Despite 20 years of societal changes, results were remarkably similar, underscoring the high degree of automaticity involved in language comprehension (large effects of 0.71 to 1.12 of a standard deviation). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
