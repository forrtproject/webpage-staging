  • Investigating the Relationship Between Guilt and Self‐control Using Experience Sampling Method in Japan: Replication of Hofmann & Fisher (2012) and Additional Investigations, Furukawa (2025). Japanese Psychological Research. Outcome recorded: mixed. Comparison of these results with those of Hofmann and Fisher's (2012) study on thematic self-control revealed that previous guilt had beneficial indirect pathways on subsequent self-control processes (goal importance, conflict, and resistance) to inhibit desire-related behavior. However, the latter reported that guilt for self-control failures had detrimental residual effects on subsequent behavioral inhibition, whereas the former did not demonstrate such a negative effect… Outcome read from the discussion.
