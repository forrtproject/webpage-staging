Discouraged by Peer Excellence

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller

Psychological Science, 2016. DOI 10.1177/0956797615623770

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Exploring the dark side of exposure to peer excellence among traditional and nontraditional college students, Cho (2019). Learning and Individual Differences. Outcome recorded: mixed. replicating the discouragement-by-exposure-to-peer-excellence effect. However, the effect was absent among nontraditional college students. Outcome read from the abstract.
