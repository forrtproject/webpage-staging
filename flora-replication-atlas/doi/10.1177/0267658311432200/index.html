  • Definite descriptions in L2 acquisition: An approximate replication of Ionin et al. (2012), Chantajinda et al. (2026). 언어연구. Outcome recorded: mixed. Overall, the results of our picture-based AJT point to a partial confirmation of the original study. Compared to L1-English speakers, L1-Korean L2-English speakers are more likely to allow the 'same' interpretation for definite plurals in anaphoric contexts (M = 2.70 for the L1-English group vs. M = 3.31 for the L2-English group).
