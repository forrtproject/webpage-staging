Intention Invention and the Affect Misattribution Procedure

B. Keith Payne, Jazmin Brown-Iannuzzi, Melissa Burkley, Nathan L. Arbuckle, Erin Cooley, C. Daryl Cameron, Kristjen B. Lundberg

Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 2013. DOI 10.1177/0146167212475225

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Effects on the Affect Misattribution Procedure are strongly moderated by influence awareness, Hughes et al. (2022). Behavior Research Methods. Outcome recorded: failed. We did not replicate the finding that standard and skip AMP effects are similar to one another. Whereas Payne et al. (2013; Experiment 3) found no difference between a skip and standard AMP, we found a difference between the two, such that scores on the standard AMP were sig- nificantly larger than those on the skip AMP (which were also in the opposite direction to what the original authors found).
