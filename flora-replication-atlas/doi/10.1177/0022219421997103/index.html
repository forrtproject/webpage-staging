A Systematic Review of CBM Professional Development Materials: Are Teachers Receiving Sufficient Instruction in Data-Based Decision-Making?

Christine A. Espin, Roxette M. van den Bosch, Marijke van der Liende, Ralph C. A. Rippe, Melissa Beutick, Athanasia Langa, Suzanne E. Mol

Journal of Learning Disabilities, 2021. DOI 10.1177/0022219421997103

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • A Systematic Review of CBM Content in Practitioner-Focused Journals: Do We Talk About Instructional Decision-Making?, Fry et al. (2023). Journal of Learning Disabilities. Outcome recorded: successful. Results revealed a pattern similar to the one found by Espin et al. with approximately 18% of the content of practitioner articles on CBM devoted to the topic of decision-making.
