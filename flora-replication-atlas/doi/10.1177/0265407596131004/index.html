Replications

  • The Concept of Shinyuu in Japan: A Replication of and Comparison to Cole and Bradac's Study on U.S. Friendship, Maeda & Ritchie (2003). Journal of Social and Personal Relationships. Outcome recorded: mixed. Study 4 demonstrated that two of the dimensions are similar to dimensions identified in Cole and Bradac's (1996) study. The third dimension, frank–supportive versus modest–self-controlled,was not found in Cole and Bradac's study, whereas the first dimension of their study, spontaneous–active versus stable–passive,was not found in the Japanese data.
