Psychological Science, 2011. DOI 10.1177/0956797611413935

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

  • Ma-Kellams PsychologSci 2011 lkBL - Talhelm - New Data Replication - 618, Talhelm et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. Unlike the original study, the interaction between culture and writing condition was not significant F(1, 433) = 1.12, p = .291, ηp2 = 0.003. (I'll refer to the interaction between culture and writing condition on the Prostitution Attitude Scale as "the main replication test.") The patterns of the means were in the opposite direction from the original study. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
