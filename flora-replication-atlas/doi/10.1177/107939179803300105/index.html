Ann K. Griffen, John W. Schuster, Timothy E. Morse

Education and Training in Mental Retardation and Developmental Disabilities, 1998. DOI 10.1177/107939179803300105

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • The influence of instructive feedback presentation schedule: A replication with children with autism spectrum disorder, Nottingham et al. (2020). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: successful. Specifically, we replicated and extended Griffen et al. (1998) by comparing a condition in which secondary targets were presented during each trial of a session, a condition in which secondary targets were presented every other trial, and a condition in which secondary targets were presented about every 4 trials.
