The Journal of Special Education, 2008. DOI 10.1177/0022466907313348

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • iPad® Video Prompting to Teach Cooking Tasks to Korean American Adolescents With Autism Spectrum Disorder, Kim & Kang (2020). Career Development and Transition for Exceptional Individuals. Outcome recorded: successful. Results indicated the correct completion of multi-step cooking tasks increased with the intervention package and was maintained seven weeks following intervention across three participants.
