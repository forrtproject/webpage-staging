Education and Training in Developmental Disabilities, 2006. DOI 10.1177/215416470604100404

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Teaching Daily Living Skills to Seven Individuals With Severe Intellectual Disabilities: A Comparison of Video Prompting to Video Modeling, Cannella-Malone et al. (2011). Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. Outcome recorded: successful. For six participants, video prompting was more effective than video modeling, which was generally ineffective.
