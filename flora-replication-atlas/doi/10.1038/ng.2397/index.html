Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • A replication study of genetic risk loci for ischemic stroke in a Dutch population: a case-control study, Hauer et al. (2017). Scientific Reports. Outcome recorded: failed. We could not replicate the previously-established associations at the 1p13.2 (TSPAN2), 6p21.1 (SUPT3H/CDC5L), 7p21.1 (HDAC9), 9p21.3 (CDKN2B-AS1), 9q34 (ABO) and 12q24 (ALDH2) loci with overall IS or its subtypes. However, all of the effect directions were consistent with the observed directions of the initial association reports except for the C allele of rs556621 at locus 6p21.1 (SUPT3H/CDC5L) (Table 1).
