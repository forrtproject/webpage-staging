Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Association of genetic variants in chromosome 17q21 and adult-onset asthma in a Chinese Han population, Qiu et al. (2011). BMC Medical Genetics. Outcome recorded: successful. Our replication study suggests that variants in 17q21 are significantly associated with risk of adult-onset asthma and gene expression in a Chinese Han population. Outcome read from the abstract.
