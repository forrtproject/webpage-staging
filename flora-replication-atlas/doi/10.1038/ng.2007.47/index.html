Polymorphism at the TNF superfamily gene TNFSF4 confers susceptibility to systemic lupus erythematosus

Deborah S Cunninghame Graham, Robert R Graham, Harinder Manku, Andrew K Wong, John C Whittaker, Patrick M Gaffney, Kathy L Moser, John D Rioux, David Altshuler, Timothy W Behrens, Timothy J Vyse

Nature Genetics, 2007. DOI 10.1038/ng.2007.47

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2008.

Replications

  • Replication of the TNFSF4 (OX40L) promoter region association with systemic lupus erythematosus, Delgado-Vega et al. (2008). Genes & Immunity. Outcome recorded: successful. This first replication study confirms the association of genetic variation in the upstream region of TNFSF4 with susceptibility to SLE. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
