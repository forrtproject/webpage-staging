A common variant of HMGA2 is associated with adult and childhood height in the general population

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2009.

Replications

  • HMGA2 Is Confirmed To Be Associated with Human Adult Height, Yang et al. (2009). Annals of Human Genetics. Outcome recorded: successful. We confirmed the association between rs1042725 in HMGA2 and adult height both in the unrelated and family‐based Caucasian populations (overall P = 4.25 × 10 −9 ). Outcome read from the abstract.
