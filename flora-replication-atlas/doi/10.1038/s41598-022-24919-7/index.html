Vasilisa Akselevich, Sharon Gilaie-Dotan

Replications

  • Positive and negative facial valence perception are modulated differently by eccentricity in the parafovea: Replication from KDEF to NimStim, Akselevich & Gilaie-Dotan (2024). Scientific Reports. Outcome recorded: successful. All our prior findings replicated and eccentricity-based modulation magnitude was smaller with NimStim (~ 16.6% accuracy reduction at 4°) than with KDEF stimuli (~ 27.3% reduction). Our current investigations support our earlier findings that for briefly presented parafoveal stimuli, positive and negative valence perception are differently affected by eccentricity and may be dissociated.
