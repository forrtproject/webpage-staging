Replications

  • Experiments from unfinished Registered Reports in the Reproducibility Project: Cancer Biology, Errington et al. (2021). eLife. Outcome recorded: successful. We independently replicated an experiment to determine the expression of the endothelial marker Tie2 in patient-derived glioblastoma neurospheres compared to a glioblastoma cell line and an endothelial cell line. This is comparable to what was reported in Supplemental Figure 11C of Ricci-Vitiani et al., 2010 and described in Protocol 1 in the Registered Report ( Chroscinski et al., 2015 ).
