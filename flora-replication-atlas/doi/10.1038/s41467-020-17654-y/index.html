Placebos without deception reduce self-report and neural measures of emotional distress

Darwin A. Guevarra, Jason S. Moser, Tor D. Wager, Ethan Kross

Nature Communications, 2020. DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-17654-y

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Changes in neural processing and evaluation of negative facial expressions after administration of an open-label placebo, Schienle et al. (2022). Scientific Reports. Outcome recorded: successful. The OLP was associated with reduced LPP amplitudes (1000–6000 ms) to anger expressions across a frontal cluster. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
