Genome-wide association scan in women with systemic lupus erythematosus identifies susceptibility variants in ITGAM, PXK, KIAA1542 and other loci

John B Harley, Marta E Alarcón-Riquelme, Lindsey A Criswell, Chaim O Jacob, Robert P Kimberly, Kathy L Moser, Betty P Tsao, Timothy J Vyse, Carl D Langefeld

Nature Genetics, 2008. DOI 10.1038/ng.81

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Different genetic effect of PXK on systemic lupus erythematosus in the Korean population, Kim et al. (2011). Rheumatology International. Outcome recorded: failed. "The association of PXK rs6445975 with SLE that observed in Caucasians was not replicated in Koreans as well as Hong Kong Chinese." Outcome read from the full text. View paper
