Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2010.

Replications

  • Association of the CBLB gene with multiple sclerosis: new evidence from a replication study in an Italian population: Table 1, Corrado et al. (2010). Journal of Medical Genetics. Outcome recorded: successful. the common allele T of rs9657904 is significantly positively associated (one-tailed p=7.35×10 -5 ) and with a comparable effect size with MS (OR=1.31, 95% CI 1.14 to 1.52). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
