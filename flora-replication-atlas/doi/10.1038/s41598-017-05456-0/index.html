Midnolin is a novel regulator of parkin expression and is associated with Parkinson's Disease

Yutaro Obara, Toru Imai, Hidenori Sato, Yuji Takeda, Takeo Kato, Kuniaki Ishii

Scientific Reports, 2017. DOI 10.1038/s41598-017-05456-0

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Midnolin is a confirmed genetic risk factor for Parkinson's disease, Obara et al. (2019). Annals of Clinical and Translational Neurology. Outcome recorded: successful. We showed the strong genetic association of MIDN with PD development in a British population and in a Japanese population, suggesting MIDN is a confirmed and universal genetic risk factor for PD.
