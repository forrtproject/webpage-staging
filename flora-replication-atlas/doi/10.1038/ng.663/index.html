A genome-wide association study identifies a susceptibility locus for refractive errors and myopia at 15q14

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • Regional replication of association with refractive error on 15q14 and 15q25 in the Age-Related Eye Disease Study cohort, Simpson et al. (2013). PubMed. Outcome recorded: mixed. attempting to replicate the most significant SNPs found in one population may not be significant in another population Outcome read from the abstract.
