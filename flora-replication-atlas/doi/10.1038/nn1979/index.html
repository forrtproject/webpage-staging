Neurocognitive correlates of liberalism and conservatism

David M Amodio, John T Jost, Sarah L Master, Cindy M Yee

Nature Neuroscience, 2007. DOI 10.1038/nn1979

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Neural Correlates of Liberalism and Conservatism in a Post-communist Country, Kremláček et al. (2019). Frontiers in Human Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: failed. We did not find a relationship between the brain activity connected to conflict monitoring and self-reported conservatism/liberalism orientation (ρ = −0.11, p = 0.297) or conservatism/liberalism validated for the USA agenda (ρ = −0.01, p = 0.910).
