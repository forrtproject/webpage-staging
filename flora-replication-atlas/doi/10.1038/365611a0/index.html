Music and spatial task performance

Frances H. Rauscher, Gordon L. Shaw, Catherine N. Ky

Nature, 1993. DOI 10.1038/365611a0

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2010.

Replications

  • Effects of prior exposure to office noise and music on aspects of working memory, Smith et al. (2010). Noise and Health. Outcome recorded: successful. The results replicated the Mozart effect and showed that it was not due to changes in mood. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
