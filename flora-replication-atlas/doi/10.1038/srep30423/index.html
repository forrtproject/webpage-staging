Skilled musicians are not subject to the McGurk effect

Alice M. Proverbio, Gemma Massetti, Ezia Rizzi, Alberto Zani

Scientific Reports, 2016. DOI 10.1038/srep30423

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Skilled musicians are indeed subject to the McGurk effect, Politzer-Ahles & Pan (2019). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to the original study's conclusion, we find that musicians do show a large and statistically significant McGurk effect and that their effect is no smaller than that of non-musicians.
