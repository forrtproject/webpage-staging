Why voters who value democracy participate in democratic backsliding

Alia Braley, Gabriel S. Lenz, Dhaval Adjodah, Hossein Rahnama, Alex Pentland

Nature Human Behaviour, 2023. DOI 10.1038/s41562-023-01594-w

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2025.

Replications

  • Replication of "Why voters who value democracy participate in democratic backsliding" in Germany's multi-party context, Freitag et al. (2025). Outcome recorded: successful. Consistent with the original findings, our replication study shows that correcting voters' misperceptions about the democratic commitment of out-partisans reduces their own willingness to subvert democratic norms, from 0.25 in the control group to 0.19 in the treatment group (on a 0-1 scale). In standardized terms, the observed treatment effect size (Cohen's d = 0.4) closely matches the original effect, exceeding it by 8%. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
