Spontaneous giving and calculated greed

David G. Rand, Joshua D. Greene, Martin A. Nowak

Nature, 2012. DOI 10.1038/nature11467

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 3 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Does Intuition Cause Cooperation?, Verkoeijen & Bouwmeester (2014). PLoS ONE. Outcome recorded: failed. the results of Experiment 1 failed to reveal an intuitive-cooperation effect. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Evaluating the replicability of social science experiments in Nature and Science between 2010 and 2015, Camerer et al. (2018). Nature Human Behaviour. Outcome recorded: failed. Rep. s1+s2 = No: the pooled first-stage and replication-sample test fails to reach significance in the same direction as the original. Outcome read from coding note: Supplementary Table 5. View paper
  • Registered Replication Report: Rand, Greene, and Nowak (2012), Bouwmeester et al. (2017). Perspectives on Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. In combination, the results of the intent-to-treat analysis and the compliant-only analysis are consistent with the presence of selection biases and the absence of a causal effect of time pressure on cooperation. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
