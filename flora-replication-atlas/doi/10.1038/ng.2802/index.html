Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • ZCWPW1 is associated with late-onset Alzheimer's disease in Han Chinese: a replication study and meta-analyses, Gao et al. (2016). Oncotarget. Outcome recorded: successful. To summarize, the rs1476679 polymorphism in ZCWPW1 is associated with LOAD in Northern Han Chinese population. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • INPP5D rs35349669 polymorphism with late-onset Alzheimer's disease: A replication study and meta-analysis, Jing et al. (2016). Oncotarget. Outcome recorded: failed. Our results showed no obvious differences in the genotypic or allelic distributions of rs35349669 polymorphism between LOAD cases and healthy controls (genotype: p = 0.167; allele: p = 0.094). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • MEF2C rs190982 polymorphism with late-onset Alzheimer's disease in Han Chinese: A replication study and meta-analyses, Tang et al. (2016). Oncotarget. Outcome recorded: failed. No difference in the genotype and allele distributions of the MEF2C rs190982 polymorphism was found between LOAD cases and healthy controls (genotype: P = 0.861; allele: P = 0.862) Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
