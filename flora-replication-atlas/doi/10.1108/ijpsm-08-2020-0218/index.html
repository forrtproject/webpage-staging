Replications

  • The role of e-leadership and team dynamics in work-from-home performance: a replication study during the COVID-19 pandemic, Koh & Law (2025). Management Review Quarterly. Outcome recorded: mixed. While the original study identified both constructs as drivers of virtual team performance in public-sector settings, our findings reveal a critical divergence: in crisis-driven SMEs, perceived team dynamics – encompassing collaboration, support, and cohesion – emerge as the strongest predictor of work-from-home (WFH) performance, with perceived e-leadership showing no significant direct effect.
