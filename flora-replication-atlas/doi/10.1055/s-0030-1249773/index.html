High Frequency Plant Regeneration from Leaf Derived Callus of High Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabinol Yielding Cannabis sativa L

Hemant Lata, Suman Chandra, Ikhlas Khan, Mahmoud ElSohly

Planta Medica, 2010. DOI 10.1055/s-0030-1249773

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Recalcitrance of Cannabis sativa to de novo regeneration; a multi-genotype replication study, Monthony et al. (2021). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: failed. The shoot induction medium failed to induce shoot organogenesis in any of the 10 cultivars tested, instead resulting in necrosis of the calli. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
