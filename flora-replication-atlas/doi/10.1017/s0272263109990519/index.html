Replications

  • Effects of Form‐Focused Practice and Feedback: A Multisite Replication Study of Yang and Lyster (2010), Mifka‐Profozic et al. (2023). Language Learning. Outcome recorded: mixed. We found no effect of group and no evidence of the superiority of either prompts or recasts in either written or oral data in either Bosnia or Italy. However, we found a steady increase in scores over time from pretest to posttests in oral data in all groups at both sites. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
