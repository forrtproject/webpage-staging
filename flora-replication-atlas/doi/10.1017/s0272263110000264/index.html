THE BOUNDS OF ADULT LANGUAGE ACQUISITION

Nick C. Ellis, Nuria Sagarra

Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 2010. DOI 10.1017/s0272263110000264

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Revisiting Blocking Effects in Second Language Learning: A Close Replication of Ellis and Sagarra (2010b), McManus et al. (2025). Language Learning. Outcome recorded: mixed. Our close replication did not confirm the initial study's findings due to performance following verb pretraining: Pretraining on verbs did not bias attention to verbs in subsequent use. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • LEARNED ATTENTION IN ADULT LANGUAGE ACQUISITION, Ellis & Sagarra (2011). Studies in Second Language Acquisition. Outcome recorded: successful. replicates and extends Ellis and Sagarra (2010) in demonstrating short- and long-term learned attention in the acquisition of temporal reference in Latin. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
