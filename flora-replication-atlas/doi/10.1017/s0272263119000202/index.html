Replications

  • Development of verb argument constructions in L2 English learners: A close replication of research question 3 in Römer and Berger (2019), Liu & Lu (2024). Studies in Second Language Acquisition. Outcome recorded: successful. Our findings were consistent with the original study in showing significant positive correlations between frequencies of the verb-VAC combinations in the BNC and in the learner subcorpora but further revealed the potential effect of topic on the learners' VAC usage.
