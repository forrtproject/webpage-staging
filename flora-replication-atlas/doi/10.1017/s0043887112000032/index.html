The Cultural Divide in Europe: Migration, Multiculturalism, and Political Trust

Lauren M. McLaren

World Politics, 2012. DOI 10.1017/s0043887112000032

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • McLaren WorldPolitics 2012 wRvv - Urbanska/Kolczynska - Secondary Data Replication - 65wm6, Parsons et al. (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "All 5 claims were replicated successfully." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
  • McLaren WorldPolitics 2012 wRvv - Kołczyńska - Secondary Data Replication, Kołczyńska et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "All analyses – the main analysis and the auxiliary analyses – indicate that concern about immigration is positively associated with distrust in the country's parliament, i.e. confirm the result in the replicated study, so the replication was successful according to SCORE criteria. Exact results are as follows: Main analysis: coefficient [b] on concern about immigration term = 0.183, SE = 0.009, p < 0.000001, t value = 21.174." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
