Crime Victimization and Political Participation

REGINA BATESON

American Political Science Review, 2012. DOI 10.1017/s0003055412000299

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • BATESON AmPoliSciRev 2012 RYKv - Boxell - Secondary Data Replication - 2k5g2, Parsons et al. (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "The coefficient on crime victimization was 0.020027 with a standard error of 0.007274, which gives a p-value of 0.005900 and is well below the alpha = .05 threshold. This claim was replicated. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
  • BATESON AmPoliSciRev 2012 RYKv - Szabelska - Secondary Data Replication, Szabelska et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "The replication included 38,020 participants with an estimate of the minimum viable sample size for the data analytic replication: 1,320. There is a significant and positive coefficient on victimization, coef = 0.154, robust SE = 0.015, p < 2.2e-16. Thus, this replication of the claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
