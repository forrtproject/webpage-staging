Inez Myin-Germeys, Lydia Krabbendam, Jelle Jolles, Philippe A. Delespaul, Jim van Os

American Journal of Psychiatry, 2002. DOI 10.1176/appi.ajp.159.3.443

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2007.

Replications

  • The relationship between cognitive dysfunction and stress sensitivity in schizophrenia, Morrens et al. (2007). Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. Outcome recorded: successful. The current study replicated previous findings [10] showing that in some instances cognitive functioning is not, and in other instances is inversely associated with momentary sensitivity to stress.
