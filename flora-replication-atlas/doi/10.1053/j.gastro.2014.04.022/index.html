Replications

  • Association of a single nucleotide polymorphism upstream of ICOS with Japanese autoimmune hepatitis type 1, Higuchi et al. (2016). Journal of Human Genetics. Outcome recorded: successful. We found that rs4325730 is associated with AIH (P=0.0173, odds ratio (OR) 1.30, 95% confidence interval (CI) 1.05–1.62). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
