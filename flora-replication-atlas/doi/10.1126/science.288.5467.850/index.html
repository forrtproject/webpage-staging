Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Cooperation through Image Scoring: A Replication, Russell et al. (2020). Games. Outcome recorded: successful. …we successfully replicated the central result of Wedekind and Milinski (2000)…In each round, the player was passively exposed to information about the past generosity of the other player. In our study, we successfully replicated the central result of WM. Participants in our replication gave significantly more money to partners with higher image scores (more generous reputations) than those with lower image scores (less generous reputations).
