Reducing the Gender Achievement Gap in College Science: A Classroom Study of Values Affirmation

Akira Miyake, Lauren E. Kost-Smith, Noah D. Finkelstein, Steven J. Pollock, Geoffrey L. Cohen, Tiffany A. Ito

Science, 2010. DOI 10.1126/science.1195996

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Replicating a self-affirmation intervention to address gender differences: Successes and challenges, Kost-Smith et al. (2012). AIP Conference Proceedings. Outcome recorded: mixed. we find similar patterns along exams and course grades, but do not observe these patterns along the FMCE. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper

Reproductions

  • Can Short Psychological Interventions Affect Educational Performance? Revisiting the Effect of Self-Affirmation Interventions, Serra-Garcia et al. (2020). Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: computationally reproducible, robustness challenges. Computational: We replicated the t statistic for the interaction effect found by Miyake et al. (2010), 3.08, and indicate where it lies on the specification curve. Outcome read from Computational: results. View paper
