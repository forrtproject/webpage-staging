Targeted enhancement of cortical-hippocampal brain networks and associative memory

Jane X. Wang, Lynn M. Rogers, Evan Z. Gross, Anthony J. Ryals, Mehmet E. Dokucu, Kelly L. Brandstatt, Molly S. Hermiller, Joel L. Voss

Science, 2014. DOI 10.1126/science.1252900

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Episodic memory improvements due to noninvasive stimulation targeting the cortical–hippocampal network: A replication and extension experiment, Hermiller et al. (2019). Brain and Behavior. Outcome recorded: successful. Similar to Wang et al., paired-associate learning was significantly improved after 1 week of active stimulation but not after 1 week of sham stimulation. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
