Blue or Red? Exploring the Effect of Color on Cognitive Task Performances

Ravi Mehta, Rui (Juliet) Zhu

Science, 2009. DOI 10.1126/science.1169144

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Failure to replicate the Mehta and Zhu (2009) color-priming effect on anagram solution times, Steele (2014). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. Outcome recorded: failed. The results did not show the Mehta and Zhu color-priming effects, even though statistical power was sufficient to detect the effect. The results call into question the existence of their color-priming effect on the solution of anagrams. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
