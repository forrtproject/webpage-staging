Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • Treatment with bexarotene, a compound that increases apolipoprotein-E, provides no cognitive benefit in mutant APP/PS1 mice, LaClair et al. (2013). Molecular Neurodegeneration. Outcome recorded: failed. this drug failed to attenuate Aβ plaques or cognitive deficits in these mice. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
