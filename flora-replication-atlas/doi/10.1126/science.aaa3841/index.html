Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Unlearning implicit social biases during sleep: A failure to replicate, Humiston & Wamsley (2019). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: failed. Contrary to the results reported by Hu et al., we found no effect of cueing on implicit bias, either immediately following the nap or one week later. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
