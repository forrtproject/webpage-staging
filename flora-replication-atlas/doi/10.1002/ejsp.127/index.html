Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • Do the disadvantaged legitimize the social system? A large-scale test of the status–legitimacy hypothesis, Brandt (2013). Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. Multilevel models revealed that the average effect across years in the United States and countries throughout the world was most often directly contrary to the status–legitimacy hypothesis or was practically zero. In short, the status–legitimacy effect is not a robust phenomenon. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
