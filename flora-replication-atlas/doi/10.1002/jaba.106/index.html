Producing meaningful improvements in problem behavior of children with autism via synthesized analyses and treatments

Gregory P. Hanley, C. Sandy Jin, Nicholas R. Vanselow, Laura A. Hanratty

Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 2014. DOI 10.1002/jaba.106

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Practical Functional Assessment: A Case Study Replication and Extension with a Child Diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder, Ferguson et al. (2020). Education and Treatment of Children. Outcome recorded: successful. The results were similar to previous research with an overall reduction in problem behavior and increases in functional communicative responses and compliance with demands. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Assessing the generality and durability of interview‐informed functional analyses and treatment, Rose & Beaulieu (2018). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: successful. We observed large reductions in problem behavior following the introduction of the function-based treatment. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
