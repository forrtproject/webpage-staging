  • Career Services at Colleges and Universities: A 30-Year Replication Study, Vinson et al. (2014). Journal of College Student Development. Outcome recorded: descriptive only. With respect to the management structure of college and university CSOs, the number of institutions offering a centralized operation has remained about the same since 1979, while decentralized CSOs have now dropped to zero. The most notable changes in CSO administrative control involved a shift away from reporting to a placement office (34% in 1979 to 1% currently) to reporting to student affairs (21% in 1979 to 71% currently), indicating an increasing philosophical match… Outcome read from the conclusion.
