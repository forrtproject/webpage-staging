Social Support as Mediator of the Stress Buffering Effect of Optimism: The Importance of Differentiating the Recipients' and Providers' Perspective

Manja Vollmann, Katja Antoniw, Freda–Marie Hartung, Britta Renner

European Journal of Personality, 2011. DOI 10.1002/per.803

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Vollmann EurJournPersonality 2011 x3KP - Hostler - New Data Replication - k5z, Hostler (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "The APIM model successfully replicated the effects of Vollmann et al. The actor effect of optimism on perceived stress was b = -0.298, z = -9.529, p < .001 (p = .000 as reported by MPlus), showing that among dyad members, those with greater optimism had lower perceived levels of stress. Thus, the replication of the claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria" Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
