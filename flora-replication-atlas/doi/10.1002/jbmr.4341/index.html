Components of the Gut Microbiome That Influence Bone Tissue-Level Strength

Marysol Luna, Jason D Guss, Laura S Vasquez-Bolanos, Macy Castaneda, Manuela Vargas Rojas, Jasmin M Strong, Denise A Alabi, Sophie D Dornevil, Jacob C Nixon, Erik A Taylor, Eve Donnelly, Xueyan Fu, M Kyla Shea, Sarah L Booth, Rodrigo Bicalho, Christopher J Hernandez

Journal of Bone and Mineral Research, 2020. DOI 10.1002/jbmr.4341

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • Effects of high dose aspartame-based sweetener on the gut microbiota and bone strength in young and aged mice, Cyphert et al. (2024). JBMR Plus. Outcome recorded: uninformative. Compared to our prior study,1 dosing with sweetener generated significant, but much smaller changes in the composition of the gut microbiota (Figure 4). Since dosing with sweetener did not result in the same microbiota across studies, our findings are unable to support or refute our prior observation that changes in the gut microbiota can increase bone tissue strength above what is seen in mice with an unaltered gut microbiota.
