Replications

  • Education and conspiracy beliefs: A replication of van Prooijen (2017), Ballová Mikušková (2023). Applied Cognitive Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. These results showed the connection of education to socioeconomic status, a feeling of control and analytic thinking (cognitive reflection, scientific reasoning) and their power to reduce conspiracy beliefs.
