A RANDOMIZED TRIAL OF MOTIVATIONAL INTERVIEWING TO IMPROVE MIDDLE SCHOOL STUDENTS' ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE

Gerald Gill Strait, Bradley H. Smith, Sam McQuillin, John Terry, Suzanne Swan, Patrick S. Malone

Journal of Community Psychology, 2012. DOI 10.1002/jcop.21511

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • MOTIVATIONAL INTERVIEWING TO IMPROVE MIDDLE SCHOOL STUDENTS' ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE: A REPLICATION STUDY, Terry et al. (2013). Journal of Community Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. As with the original MI study, there was a positive main effect on math grades. Effect sizes on academic behavioral outcomes were also similar. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
