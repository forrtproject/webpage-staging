Replications

  • Further evidence of renewal in automatically maintained behavior, Falligant et al. (2024). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated their findings by retrospectively examining renewal across various topographies of automatically maintained behavior treated at an inpatient hospital, and we extended their work by also examining differences across subtypes of automatically maintained self-injurious behavior.
