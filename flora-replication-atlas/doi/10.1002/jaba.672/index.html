Colin Muething, Nathan Call, Alexis Pavlov, Joel Ringdahl, Scott Gillespie, Seth Clark, Joanna Lomas Mevers

Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 2020. DOI 10.1002/jaba.672

Replications

  • Prevalence of renewal of problem behavior: Replication and extension to an inpatient setting, Falligant et al. (2020). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: successful. Their sample was obtained exclusively from an outpatient setting and despite the applied relevance of renewal, its clinical prevalence in other populations is unknown. Accordingly, we replicated Muething et al.'s procedures and analyzed renewal in 37 inpatient treatment applications across 34 cases via consecutive-controlled case series.
