  • Machine learning to analyze single-case graphs: a replication and extension with nonsimulated data, Lanovaz (2027). Journal of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapy. Outcome recorded: mixed. Taken together, these results underline the promising nature of the support vector classifier for analyzing single-case data and would merit further attention in future research. Interestingly, the stochastic gradient descent classifier produced considerably more Type I errors than the conservative dual-criteria method, which is contrary to the pattern observed by Lanovaz and Hranchuk (2021).
