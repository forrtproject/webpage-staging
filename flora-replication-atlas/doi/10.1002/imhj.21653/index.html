Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Protective factors that buffer against the intergenerational transmission of trauma from mothers to young children: A replication study of angels in the nursery, Narayan et al. (2019). Development and Psychopathology. Outcome recorded: successful. Angel memories significantly moderated associations between maltreatment and PTSD (but not depression) symptoms, comorbid psychopathology, and children's trauma exposure.
