Timothy Werner

Strategic Management Journal, 2017. DOI 10.1002/smj.2682

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Reexamining investor reaction to covert corporate political activity: A replication and extension of Werner (2017), Minefee et al. (2020). Strategic Management Journal. Outcome recorded: mixed. Werner found that investors reacted positively to the accidental disclosure of covert ties to the Republican Governors Association. In contrast, when we apply the same research design to the ALEC leak, we find that, on average, firms tied to ALEC experienced negative abnormal returns. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
