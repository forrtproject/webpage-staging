Replications

  • Comparing interteaching and discussion forums in an asynchronous online classroom: A replication, Shaw et al. (2026). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: mixed. The results of the current study showed that participants generally scored higher on quizzes in the interteaching condition than in the discussion forum condition. However, differentiated effects did not generalize to project scores. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
