The Relationship of Handwriting Speed, Working Memory, Language Comprehension and Outlines to Lecture Note‐taking and Test‐taking among College Students

Stephen T. Peverly, Pooja C. Vekaria, Lindsay A. Reddington, James F. Sumowski, Kamauru R. Johnson, Crystal M. Ramsay

Applied Cognitive Psychology, 2012. DOI 10.1002/acp.2881

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • The Lecture Note-Taking Skills of Adolescents With and Without Learning Disabilities, Oefinger & Peverly (2020). Journal of Learning Disabilities. Outcome recorded: successful. Results of regression analyses indicated that note-taking (f(2) = 1.94) and test-taking (f(2) = 2.69) were associated with listening comprehension and background knowledge predominately.
