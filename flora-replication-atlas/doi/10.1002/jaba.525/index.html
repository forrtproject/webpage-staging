Relative preferences for edible and leisure stimuli in children with autism

Daniel E. Conine, Timothy R. Vollmer

Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 2018. DOI 10.1002/jaba.525

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Relative preferences for edible and leisure stimuli in children with autism: A replication in Italy, Slanzi et al. (2019). Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis. Outcome recorded: successful. Results of our study were similar: For 44% of participants, at least one leisure item ranked above all edible items and leisure items displaced all edible items for 28% of participants.
