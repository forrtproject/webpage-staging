The effect of background music and noise on the cognitive test performance of introverts and extraverts

Stacey Dobbs, Adrian Furnham, Alastair McClelland

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • The effect of background music and noise on the cognitive test performance of Chinese introverts and extraverts, Kou et al. (2017). Psychology of Music. Outcome recorded: failed. The results did not reveal any differences in performance as a function of the distraction condition, nor was there a difference in performance between extraverts and introverts.
