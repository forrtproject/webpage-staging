Intonation and auditory grouping in immediate serial recall

Clive Frankish

Applied Cognitive Psychology, 1995. DOI 10.1002/acp.2350090703

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Intonation does aid serial recall after all, Savino et al. (2020). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. Outcome recorded: failed. Our results show that when using naturalistic intonation contours, intonation facilitates serial recall as compared with simple pause grouping, especially for items in specific positions: end of first and second triplets, and end of the whole sequence.
