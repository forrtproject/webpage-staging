Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • Correcting vaccine misinformation: A failure to replicate familiarity or fear-driven backfire effects, Ecker et al. (2023). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: failed. None of the conditions increased vaccine misconceptions relative to control at either timepoint, or relative to a pre-intervention baseline; thus, no backfire effects were observed.
