Mirror-Induced Behavior in the Magpie (Pica pica): Evidence of Self-Recognition

Helmut Prior, Ariane Schwarz, Onur Güntürkün

PLoS Biology, 2008. DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.0060202

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.
