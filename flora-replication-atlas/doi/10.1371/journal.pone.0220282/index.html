PLOS ONE, 2019. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0220282

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • The relation between Self-Control, Need for Cognition and Action Orientation in secondary school students: A conceptual replication study, Colling et al. (2023). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: mixed. In line with the results in university students, Self-Control was predicted by NFC and AO. The model furthermore identified both constructs as correlated predictors of Self-Control. In addition, AO partially mediated the relation between NFC and Self-Control with the direct relation being stronger than the indirect one through AO.
