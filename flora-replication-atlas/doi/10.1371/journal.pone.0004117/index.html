Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Does sleep protect memories against interference? A failure to replicate, Bailes et al. (2020). PLOS ONE. Outcome recorded: failed. We were unable to replicate the finding that sleep protects memories against retroactive interference, with the detrimental effects of interference learning being essentially the same in wake and sleep participants.
