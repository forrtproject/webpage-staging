Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Masked Syllable Priming Effects in Word and Picture Naming in Chinese, You et al. (2012). PLoS ONE. Outcome recorded: successful. These four experiments consistently found that CV targets were named faster when preceded by CV primes than when they were preceded by CVG, CVN or unrelated primes. Outcome read from the abstract.
