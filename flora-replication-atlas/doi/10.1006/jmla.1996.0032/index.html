Word Segmentation: The Role of Distributional Cues

Jenny R. Saffran, Elissa L. Newport, Richard N. Aslin

Journal of Memory and Language, 1996. DOI 10.1006/jmla.1996.0032

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Replication of Saffran, Newport, & Aslin (1996) Word segmentation: The role of distributional cues, Exp. 1, Murdock et al. (2018). OSF Preprints (OSF Preprints). Outcome recorded: mixed. We were able to replicate SNA96's finding of above-chance recognition of words in a statistical learning paradigm. However, we are not able to conclude that this learning would generalize to other stimuli – a question that SNA96 did not address. We were unable to confirm any of SNA96's other observations. This is despite having a sample size over 4x larger than the original. Outcome read from the discussion.
