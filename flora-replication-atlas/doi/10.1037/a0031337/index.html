The negative testing effect and multifactor account

Daniel J. Peterson, Neil W. Mulligan

Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 2013. DOI 10.1037/a0031337

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed, 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • The replicability of the negative testing effect: Differences across participant populations, Mulligan et al. (2018). Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition. Outcome recorded: mixed. (i.e., the negative testing effect was found); for the KSU sample, there was no difference on either measure. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Does testing impair relational processing? Failed attempts to replicate the negative testing effect, Rawson et al. (2015). Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition. Outcome recorded: failed. did not replicate the negative testing effect on final memory performance nor on most measures of interitem relational processing. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
