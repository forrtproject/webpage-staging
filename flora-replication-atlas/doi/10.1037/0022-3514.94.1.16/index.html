Why do implicit and explicit attitude tests diverge? The role of structural fit

B. Keith Payne, Melissa A. Burkley, Mark B. Stokes

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2008. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.94.1.16

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Many Labs 5: Testing Pre-Data-Collection Peer Review as an Intervention to Increase Replicability, Ebersole et al. (2020). Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. 95% CI for the meta-analytic replication includes 0 under both the RP:P and Revised Protocols (effect not significant in the same direction as the original under either). Outcome read from coding note: Table 3. View paper
  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: successful. The present replication of Study 4 from Payne et al. (2008) successfully replicated the original results. Outcome read from conclusion section in the repository. View paper
  • Many Labs 5: Registered Replication of Payne, Burkley, and Stokes (2008), Study 4, Ebersole et al. (2020). Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Teams from eight universities, four in the United States and four in Italy, replicated the original study (replication N = 1,103). Although we did detect moderation by the sample's country, it was due to a reversal of the original effect in the United States and a lack of the original effect in Italy. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
