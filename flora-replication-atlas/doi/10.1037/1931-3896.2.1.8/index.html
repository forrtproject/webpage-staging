How shape constancy relates to drawing accuracy

Dale J. Cohen, Holly Earls Jones

Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, 2008. DOI 10.1037/1931-3896.2.1.8

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Does Shape Constancy Relate to Drawing Ability? Two Failures to Replicate, McManus et al. (2011). Empirical Studies of the Arts. Outcome recorded: failed. Study 1 found a correlation that was statistically significant, but in the opposite direction to that reported by Cohen and Jones, and in Study 2 the correlation was very close to zero.
