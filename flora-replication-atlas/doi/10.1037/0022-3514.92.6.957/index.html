Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • Failures to change stimulus evaluations by means of subliminal approach and avoidance training, Van Dessel et al. (2016). Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. Three experiments, including a direct replication of the study by Kawakami et al. (2007), failed to provide any evidence for effects of subliminal AA training on implicit or explicit evaluations.
