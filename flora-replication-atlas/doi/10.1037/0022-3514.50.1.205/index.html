The central question of this study was to examine whether gender-schematic processing can be well measured by means of a sex-role inventory, by conducting an extended replication study of the results of Larsen and Seidman (1986), using a Dutch sample and the GRAS, a Dutch sex-role inventory. Generally speaking, the present data suggest an affirmative answer.
