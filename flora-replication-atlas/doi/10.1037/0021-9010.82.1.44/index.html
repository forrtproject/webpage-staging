Replications

  • In Search of the Elusive U-Shaped Performance-Turnover Relationship: Are High Performing Swiss Bankers More Liable to Quit?, Salamin & Hom (2005). Journal of Applied Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. Survival regression revealed that performance is curvilinearly related to quits and that bonus pay deterred superior performers from leaving more than did pay increases. Further, the average number of job levels advanced per promotion rather than promotion rate increased quit risks. --- " This analysis replicated Trevor et al.'s (1997) finding that wage growth deters turnover.
