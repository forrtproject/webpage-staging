Jessica L. Tracy, Richard W. Robins

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2008. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.94.3.516

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. Consistent with some of the findings of Tracy and Robins (2008), recognition rates for all pride expressions were significantly greater than chance. This is a full replication of the main result. However, our study did not find a significant main effect of gender found in Study 3 of Tracy and Robins (2008). This result did not replicate the results of the Study 3 in which target gender influenced pride recognition and the perceived intensity of pride.
