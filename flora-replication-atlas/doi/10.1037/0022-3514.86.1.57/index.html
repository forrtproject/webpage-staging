Gerben A. van Kleef, Carsten K. W. De Dreu, Antony S. R. Manstead

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2004. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.57

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2017.

Replications

  • Boosheid en blijdschap in onderhandelingen, Pollmann et al. (2017). Tijdschrift voor Communicatiewetenschap. Outcome recorded: successful. In an independent direct replication we find almost the same pattern of results as the original study. We conclude that the effect is of medium size and robust. Outcome read from the abstract.
