Replications

  • Weinstein (1984): Replication and extension, Chan & Feldman (2020). Outcome recorded: successful. In the current replication study, we found support for the original findings in the target article for comparative risk judgments, yet with weaker effects (Cohen's d = -0.40, 95% CI [-0.59, -0.22]). Additionally, the current replication also found support for the Optimism bias effect across five risk categories (1. Actions / 2. Heredity / 3. Physical/physiological / 4. Environmental / 5.Psychlogical in all 12 of the hypothetical health and safety scenarios. Outcome read from the abstract.
