Keith Rayner, David A. Balota, Alexander Pollatsek

Canadian Journal of Psychology / Revue canadienne de psychologie, 1986. DOI 10.1037/h0080111

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Lack of semantic parafoveal preview benefit in reading revisited, Rayner et al. (2014). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. Outcome recorded: successful. "Thus, the present experiment replicates the earlier data reported by Rayner et al. (1986), indicating evidence for an orthographic preview benefit but a lack of semantic preview benefit in reading English." Outcome read from the full text. View paper
