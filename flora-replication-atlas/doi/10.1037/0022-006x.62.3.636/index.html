The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2000.

Replications

  • Forgetting child abuse: Feldman-Summers and Pope's (1994) study replicated among Dutch psychologists, Westerhof et al. (2000). Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy. Outcome recorded: successful.
