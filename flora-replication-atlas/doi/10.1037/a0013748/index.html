Moral typecasting: Divergent perceptions of moral agents and moral patients

Kurt Gray, Daniel M. Wegner

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2009. DOI 10.1037/a0013748

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Many Labs 2: Investigating Variation in Replicability Across Samples and Settings, Klein et al. (2018). Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: successful. This result is consistent with the hypothesis that an adult's perceived responsibility for harming a baby is greater than a baby's perceived responsibility for harming an adult. The effect size in the replication was slightly larger than the original result (d = 0.95, 95% CI = [0.91, 1.00], vs. original d = 0.80). Outcome read from the results. View paper
