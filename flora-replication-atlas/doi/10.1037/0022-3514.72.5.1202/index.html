Replications

  • The association between parental generativity and parent-child attitude-similarity through parent- and child-reported authoritative parenting: A replication, Busch (2023). Europe's Journal of Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. In other words, the more generative parents were, the more authoritative parenting they reported (cf. Pratt et al., 2001). Parents' reports on authoritative parenting were associated with their children's perception of authoritative parenting (cf. Lehmann et al., 2021). Finally, the more authoritative parenting children reported, the more similar they judged their attitudes to be to their parents'.
