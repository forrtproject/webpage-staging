The relation between perception and behavior, or how to win a game of Trivial Pursuit

Ap Dijksterhuis, Ad van Knippenberg

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1998. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.74.4.865

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Registered Replication Report: Dijksterhuis and van Knippenberg (1998), O'Donnell et al. (2018). Perspectives on Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. We observed no overall difference in trivia performance between participants primed with the "professor" category and those primed with the "hooligan" category (0.14%) and no moderation by gender.
