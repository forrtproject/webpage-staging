Replications

  • Are affective factors related to individual differences in facial expression recognition?, Alharbi et al. (2020). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. Palermo et al. (2018) recently reported that individuals who scored lower in anxiety performed significantly better on two measures of facial-expression recognition, but not a third measure. By contrast, facial-expression recognition was not significantly correlated with measures of depression, positive or negative affect, empathy, or autistic-like traits. Outcome read from the abstract.
