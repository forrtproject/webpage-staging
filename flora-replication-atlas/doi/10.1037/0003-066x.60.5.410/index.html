Positive Psychology Progress: Empirical Validation of Interventions

Martin E. P. Seligman, Tracy A. Steen, Nansook Park, Christopher Peterson

American Psychologist, 2005. DOI 10.1037/0003-066x.60.5.410

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed, 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Do Positive Psychology Exercises Work? A Replication of Seligman et al. (), Mongrain & Anselmo-Matthews (2012). Journal of Clinical Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. The PPEs did not exceed the control condition in producing changes in depression over time. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • A Character Strengths Intervention for Happiness and Depression in Saudi Arabia: A Replication of Seligman et al.'s (2005) Study, Basurrah et al. (2020). Outcome recorded: failed. all participants, independent of the condition, reported being happier and less depressed (p < .005) post-intervention. Outcome read from the abstract.
