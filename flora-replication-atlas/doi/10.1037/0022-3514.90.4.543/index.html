What do i think you're doing? Action identification and mind attribution

Megan N. Kozak, Abigail A. Marsh, Daniel M. Wegner

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2006. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.90.4.543

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Kozak et al 2006 replication and extension, Hin & Feldman (2021). OSF Registries. Outcome recorded: mixed. In the replication of Study 1, we failed to find support for the positive correlation between the number of high level action identification and overall mind attribution (original: r = .3, 90% CI [.024, .53]; replication: r = .08, 90% CI [-.07, .24]).
