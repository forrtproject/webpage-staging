The testing effect in recognition memory: A dual process account

Jason C. K. Chan, Kathleen B. McDermott

Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 2007. DOI 10.1037/0278-7393.33.2.431

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2011.

Replications

  • Comparing the Effects of Testing and Restudying on Recollection in Recognition Memory, Verkoeijen et al. (2011). Experimental Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. First, we replicated the outcomes reported by Chan and McDermott (2007) in their third experiment. Specifically, we showed that testing increases the relative contribution of recollection processes in recognition performance, at least when compared to studying items once.
