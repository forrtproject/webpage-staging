Functional equivalence of verbal and spatial information in serial short-term memory

Dylan Jones, Paul Farrand, George Stuart, Neil Morris

Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 1995. DOI 10.1037/0278-7393.21.4.1008

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Registered Replication Report: Testing Disruptive Effects of Irrelevant Speech on Visual-Spatial Working Memory, Kvetnaya (2018). Journal of European Psychology Students. Outcome recorded: failed. In contrast to the original findings, no main effect of sound type was observed, F(2, 78) = 0.81, p = .450, η²_p = .02. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • A Replication of "Functional Equivalence of Verbal and Spatial Information in Serial Short-Term Memory (1995; Experiments 2 and 3)", Guitard & Saint-Aubin (2015). The Quantitative Methods for Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. In summary, our results confirm the presence of interference across domains, but show that this interference is of smaller magnitude than withindomain interference. Our results further show the risk of arguing for the null effect—as it was the case of Jones et al. (1995) with their non-significant interaction—in the context of null hypothesis testing. Outcome read from p. 4, General Discussion. View paper
