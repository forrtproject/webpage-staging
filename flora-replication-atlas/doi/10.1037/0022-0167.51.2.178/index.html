Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2008.

Replications

  • Moderators of the body dissatisfaction-eating disorder symptomatology relationship: Replication and extension, Brannan & Petrie (2008). Journal of Counseling Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. As expected, body dissatisfaction was strongly related to the measures of disordered eating, accounting for 16% to 26% of the variance. Outcome read from the abstract.
