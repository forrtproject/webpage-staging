Cynthia C. Woo, Michael Leon

Behavioral Neuroscience, 2013. DOI 10.1037/a0033010

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Environmental enrichment as a therapy for autism: A clinical trial replication and extension, Woo et al. (2015). Behavioral Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: successful. After 6 months, enriched children showed statistically significant gains in their IQ scores, a decline in their atypical sensory responses, and an improvement in their receptive language performance, compared to controls.
