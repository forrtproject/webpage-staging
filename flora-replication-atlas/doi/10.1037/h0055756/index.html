Forming impressions of personality

S. E. Asch

The Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology, 1946. DOI 10.1037/h0055756

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 3 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 mixed, 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • The Primacy Effect in Impression Formation: Some Replications and Extensions, Sullivan (2018). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. we found support for a small primacy effect in impression formation, but found no evidence of a pragmatic explanation for primacy effects. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Forming Impressions of Personality, Nauts et al. (2014). Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. We found no evidence for a primacy-of-warmth effect. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • First impression replication study (Asch, 1945, Experiment 1), Pavlović & Kaiser (2024). OSF. Outcome recorded: mixed. No interpretation reported, only statistical results - yet they are mixed Outcome read from the abstract.
