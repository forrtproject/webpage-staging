Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: uninformative. While this study mirrored the original protocol, it failed to illicit enough responses to draw meaningful conclusions regarding the interaction between commitment and letter focus. Based on the lack of response, further investigation should be conducted with more respondents to test these findings. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
