Inaction inertia: Foregoing future benefits as a result of an initial failure to act

Orit E. Tykocinski, Thane S. Pittman, Erin E. Tuttle

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1995. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.68.5.793

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Foregone Opportunities and Choosing Not to Act: Replications of Inaction Inertia Effect, Chen et al. (2020). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. We found consistent findings across samples, with the inaction inertia effect dependent on the scenario used. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper

Meta-Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed.
