Replications

  • Optimal task-sets override attentional capture by rare cues, Schönhammer & Kerzel (2018). Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance. Outcome recorded: mixed. Experiment 1 replicated Folk and Remington's (2015) Experiment 3 in that rare onset cues elicited validity effects, even though the target was a red feature target. Hence, the attention-driving signal elicited by rare onset cues outweighed the task-set for red color, and the cues consequently captured attention against observers' goals.
