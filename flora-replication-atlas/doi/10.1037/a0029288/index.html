Mere exposure to money increases endorsement of free-market systems and social inequality

Eugene M. Caruso, Kathleen D. Vohs, Brittani Baxter, Adam Waytz

Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 2013. DOI 10.1037/a0029288

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Do subtle reminders of money change people's political views?, Rohrer et al. (2015). Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. Outcome recorded: failed. We conducted high-powered replication attempts of these 4 money priming effects and found no evidence of priming (weighted Cohen's d = 0.03). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Investigating Variation in Replicability, Klein et al. (2014). Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. In the aggregate, 10 effects replicated consistently. One effect – imagined contact reducing prejudice – showed weak support for replicability. And two effects – flag priming influencing conservatism and currency priming influencing system justification – did not replicate. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
