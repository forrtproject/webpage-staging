Developmental and individual differences in pure numerical estimation

Julie L. Booth, Robert S. Siegler

Developmental Psychology, 2006. DOI 10.1037/0012-1649.41.6.189

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2016.

Replications

  • Numerical Estimation in Children for Both Positive and Negative Numbers, Brez et al. (2016). Journal of Cognition and Development. Outcome recorded: successful. We replicated the shift from a logarithmic to linear representation for positive numbers (0–1,000 scale) in that 2nd graders represented positive numbers logarithmically, but 4th and 6th graders represented the numbers linearly.
