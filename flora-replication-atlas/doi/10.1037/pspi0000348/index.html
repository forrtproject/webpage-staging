The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Replication of a study from "Counterfeit Diversity: How Strategically Misrepresenting Gender Diversity Dampens Organizations' Perceived Sincerity and Elevates Women's Identity Threat Concerns" (JPSP | Kroeper, Williams & Murphy 2022), Replications & Harris (2022). Clearer Thinking. Outcome recorded: mixed. Our replication study had findings that were mostly consistent with the original study. One interesting difference was that, in our study, the indirect effects of diversity condition on company interest via "perceived sincerity" were not mediated by participant gender (unlike in the original study).
