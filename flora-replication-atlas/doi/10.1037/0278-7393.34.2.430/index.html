Across-notation automatic numerical processing

Dana Ganor-Stern, Joseph Tzelgov

Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 2008. DOI 10.1037/0278-7393.34.2.430

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: successful. In line with Ganor-Stern & Tzelgov (2008), we found a CDE when the task required numerical matching. We were also able to replicate the value interference effect in the physical matching task, which "suggest[s] that automatic numerical processing was taking place" (p.436) Outcome read from p. 4.
