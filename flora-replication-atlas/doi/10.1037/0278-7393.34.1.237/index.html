Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: mixed. We performed a close replication of Experiment 2 by Mitchell and colleagues (2008) and partially replicated their results. We find a similar main effect of same versus different trials, but no main effect of training condition (AX/BX versus CX). Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
