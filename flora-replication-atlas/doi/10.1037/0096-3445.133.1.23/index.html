Music, Pandas, and Muggers: On the Affective Psychology of Value

Christopher K. Hsee, Yuval Rottenstreich

Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 2004. DOI 10.1037/0096-3445.133.1.23

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Hsee and Rottenstreich (2004) replication & extension, Feldman & Tam (2021). OSF Registries. Outcome recorded: mixed. Replicating their Study 2, we found support for the hypothesis that receiving affect-rich music books was less affected by monetary value compared to affect-poor situation of receiving mere cash (original: η 2 = 0.02, 90% CI [0.003, 0.05]; replication: η2 = 0.01, 90% CI [0.003, 0.03]).
