Military sexual trauma is associated with eating disorders, while combat exposure is not

Jessica Y. Breland, Rosemary Donalson, Yongmei Li, Claire L. Hebenstreit, Lizabeth A. Goldstein, Shira Maguen

Psychological Trauma: Theory, Research, Practice, and Policy, 2018. DOI 10.1037/tra0000276

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Sexual trauma uniquely associated with eating disorders: A replication study, Gomez et al. (2021). Psychological Trauma: Theory, Research, Practice, and Policy. Outcome recorded: successful. Using a cross-sectional logistic regression model, sexual trauma was the only independent predictor of EDs in the model, thus replicating the findings of Breland et al. (2018) in a different population. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
