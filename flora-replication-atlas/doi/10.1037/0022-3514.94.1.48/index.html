1/f noise and effort on implicit measures of bias

Joshua Correll

Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2008. DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.94.1.48

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Making sense of the noise: Replication difficulties of Correll's (2008) modulation of 1/f noise in a racial bias task, Madurski & LeBel (2014). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. Outcome recorded: failed. Despite considerable effort to closely duplicate all procedural and methodological details of the original study (i.e., same cover story, experimental manipula- tion, implicit measure task, original stimuli, task instructions, sampling frame, population, and statistical analyses), both replication attempts were unsuccessful in replicating the original finding challenging the theoretical account that 1/f noise in racial bias tasks reflects a deliberative process. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science, Collaboration (2015). Science. Outcome recorded: failed. Coded as not replicated: Replicate = 0 (the project's binary success indicator combining significance and direction). Outcome read from coding note: data file (osf.io). View paper
