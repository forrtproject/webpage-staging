Replications

  • Many Labs 3: Evaluating participant pool quality across the academic semester via replication, Ebersole et al. (2016). Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Across the replication studies (N = 3,279), participants took longer to categorize incongruent words than congruent words (M = 0.27, SD = 0.31), t(3,336) = 50.22, p b .001, d = .88, 95% CI = [.84, .92].
