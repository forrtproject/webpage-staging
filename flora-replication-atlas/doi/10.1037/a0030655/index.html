When cheating would make you a cheater: Implicating the self prevents unethical behavior

Christopher J. Bryan, Gabrielle S. Adams, Benoît Monin

Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 2013. DOI 10.1037/a0030655

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Stage 2 Registered Report: How subtle linguistic cues prevent unethical behaviors, Guo et al. (2020). F1000Research. Outcome recorded: successful. In Experiment 1, we successfully replicated the results of the original experiment. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
  • To be an honest person or not to be a cheater: Replicating the effect of messages relating to the self on unethical behaviour, Savir & Gamliel (2018). International Journal of Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. A meta-analysis of the five experiments revealed a small effect of the self-appeals relative to the behaviour-appeals. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
