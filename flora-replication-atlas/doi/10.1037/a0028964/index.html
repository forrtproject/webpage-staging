Development of muscularity and weight concerns in heterosexual and sexual minority males

Jerel P. Calzo, Heather L. Corliss, Emily A. Blood, Alison E. Field, S. Bryn Austin

Health Psychology, 2013. DOI 10.1037/a0028964

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Calzo HealthPsych 2013 gRWz - Le Forestier - New Data Replication, Forestier et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: mixed. "Although the primary model did not yield a significant result consistent with that which was reported in Calzo et al. (2013), a significant effect was found in a pre-registered follow-up analysis using a demographic subset of the sample that more closely resembled that of the original study: White men. The effect was very small, but achieved statistical significance in the Phase 2 dataset nonetheless. The effect may therefore generalize primarily to White men." Outcome read from OSF report.
