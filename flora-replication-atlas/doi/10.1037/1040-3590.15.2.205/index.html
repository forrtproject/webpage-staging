Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2012.

Replications

  • Personality-based latent classes of posttraumatic psychopathology: Personality disorders and the internalizing/externalizing model, Wolf et al. (2012). Journal of Abnormal Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Results suggest that a 3-class solution reflecting internalizing, externalizing, and simple subtypes of posttraumatic psychopathology provided the best fit to the data.
