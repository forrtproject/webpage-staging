Replications

  • Visual search with many targets in mind: A successful replication of Drew, Boettcher, and Wolfe (2017), de Leeuw et al. (2026). Center for Open Science. Outcome recorded: successful. Our replication (N=38) finds strong agreement with the original (N=14) results. As the number of memorized objects increases, participants fixate longer on each individual object.
