SUPERSTITIOUS KEY PECKING AFTER THREE PECK‐PRODUCED REINFORCEMENTS

Allen J. Neuringer

Journal of the Experimental Analysis of Behavior, 1970. DOI 10.1901/jeab.1970.13-127

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 1989.

Replications

  • Superstitious Conditioning: A Replication and Extension of Neuringer (1970), Gleeson et al. (1989). The Psychological Record. Outcome recorded: successful. After immediate, response-dependent reinforcers were delivered for the first three key pecks made by experimentally naive pigeons, a variable-time schedule of response-independent reinforcement maintained key pecking. This result also was reported by Neuringer (1970). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
