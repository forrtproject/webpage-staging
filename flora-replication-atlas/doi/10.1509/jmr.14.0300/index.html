Sounds Big: The Effects of Acoustic Pitch on Product Perceptions

Michael L. Lowe, Kelly L. Haws

Journal of Marketing Research, 2017. DOI 10.1509/jmr.14.0300

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Lowe JournMarketRes 2017 VGAe - Hannon - New Data Replication, Hannon et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. A one-way between-subjects ANOVA found no main effect of voice-pitch condition [F(1, 613) = 2.53, p= 0.112, Cohen's f2= 0.004], such that the high-voice (n= 301, M= 5.09, SD= 0.94) and low-voice conditions (n= 314, M= 5.21, SD= 1.04) were not significantly different in their sandwich size ratings.Thus, after stage 2, the replication remained unsuccessful according to SCORE criteria.
