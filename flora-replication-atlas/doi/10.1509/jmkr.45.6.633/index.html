Replications

  • Registered Replication Report on Mazar, Amir, and Ariely (2008), Verschuere et al. (2018). Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: failed. In the primary meta-analysis (19 replications, total n = 4,674), participants who were given an opportunity to cheat reported solving 0.11 more matrices if they were given a moral reminder than if they were given a neutral reminder (95% confidence interval = [−0.09, 0.31]). This small effect was numerically in the opposite direction of the effect observed in the original study (Cohen's d = −0.04). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
