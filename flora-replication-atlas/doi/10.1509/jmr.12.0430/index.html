Framing Influences Willingness to Pay but Not Willingness to Accept

Yang Yang, Joachim Vosgerau, George Loewenstein

Journal of Marketing Research, 2013. DOI 10.1509/jmr.12.0430

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2025.

Replications

  • SCORE new data replication of Yang et al. (2013), Tyner et al. (2025). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "The mean WTP in the giftcard condition (0.1202209, n = 320) was statistically significantly higher than the mean WTP in the lottery condition (-0.1217427, n = 316): t = 3.081, df = 626.2, p = 0.002154. Thus, this replication of the claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria. The Cohen's D effect size is 0.2441509." Outcome read from OSF report.

Reproductions

  • Yang JournMarketRes 2013 G1Lr - Somo - Reproduction (with author data & code), Miske et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: computationally reproducible, not checked.
