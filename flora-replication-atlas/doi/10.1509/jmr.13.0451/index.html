Meng Zhu, Rebecca K. Ratner

Journal of Marketing Research, 2015. DOI 10.1509/jmr.13.0451

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Empirical audit and review and an assessment of evidentiary value in research on the psychological consequences of scarcity, O'Donnell et al. (2021). Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. Outcome recorded: failed. Results suggested that there was no significant difference between the scarce (M = 2.92, SD = 0.96) and abundant (M = 2.86, SD = 1.04) conditions on the number of different vegetables chosen, ZMann–Whitney = -0.22, p = 0.83.
