The color red reduces snack food and soft drink intake

Oliver Genschow, Leonie Reutner, Michaela Wänke

Appetite, 2012. DOI 10.1016/j.appet.2011.12.023

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Can the color red trick you into drinking less? A replication study, Dolezalova et al. (2021). Appetite. Outcome recorded: failed. "Unlike in the previous study, the findings in our replication study did not provide empirical support for the hypothesis that people will drink less from red-labeled cups than blue-labeled cups. The difference between groups in drink consumption was non-significant. Thus, the red color did not have an inhibitory effect on drink intake." View paper
