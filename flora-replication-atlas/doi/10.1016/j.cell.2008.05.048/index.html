Replications

  • CALHM1 P86L Polymorphism is Associated with Late-Onset Alzheimer's Disease in a Recessive Model, Boada et al. (2010). Journal of Alzheimer's Disease. Outcome recorded: mixed. By applying a recessive model, we observed weak evidence of an association between P86L mutation and late-onset AD (LOAD) susceptibility in our case-control study (OR =1.38 C.I. = [1.01-1.89]).
