Replications

  • Learning New Response Sequences: A PORTL Replication and Extension of Reid 1994, Harris (2025). Outcome recorded: successful. Reid showed that rats learned a new sequence faster when the last element in the sequence was changed than when the first element was changed. The present study replicated Reid's work using human participants and a tabletop game called PORTL. Similar to Reid, participants were periodically shifted to a new target sequence that differed in either the first or last position. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
