Fertility and race perception predict voter preference for Barack Obama

Carlos David Navarrete, Melissa M. McDonald, Michael L. Mott, Joseph Cesario, Robert Sapolsky

Evolution and Human Behavior, 2010. DOI 10.1016/j.evolhumbehav.2010.05.002

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Navarrete EvoHumanBehavior 2010 1X9W - Edlund - New Data Replication, Edlund et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. "The H effect did not replicate as there was no significant relationship between conception risk and voting preference using the coding scheme used by Navarrete et al, r(226) = -.086, p = .195. The H effect did not replicate as there was significant relationship in the opposite direction between conception risk and voting preference using the coding scheme used by Navarrete et al, r(545) = -.112, p = .009" Outcome read from OSF report.
