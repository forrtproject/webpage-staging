Hot under the collar in a lukewarm environment: Words associated with hot temperature increase aggressive thoughts and hostile perceptions

C. Nathan DeWall, Brad J. Bushman

Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 2009. DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2009.05.003

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • The Effect of "Heat-Priming" on Hostile Perceptions, McCarthy (2014). OSF Registries. Outcome recorded: failed. These replication attempts failed to find any evidence that exposure to heat primes affected hostile perceptions relative to the cold primes (ds b −0.06) or neutral primes (ds b 0.00).
