Using implicit goal priming to improve the quality of self-report data

Kenneth A. Rasinski, Penny S. Visser, Maria Zagatsky, Edith M. Rickett

Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 2005. DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2004.07.001

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • Can the goal of honesty be primed?, Pashler et al. (2013). Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. Experiment 1 repeated the study with a sample of 150 students. No priming effects were observed here, nor in a follow-up study using adults recruited on the web (Experiment 2). Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
