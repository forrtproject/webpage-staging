William L. Dunlop, Tara P. McCoy, Omar Staben

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • From goals to traits: A pre-registered replication study of Dunlop, McCoy, and Staben (2017), Stewart et al. (2022). Journal of Research in Personality. Outcome recorded: successful. The present study replicated Dunlop and colleagues' findings, which carries two implications. One: that brief descriptions of what one typically tries to accomplish each day are an additional valid source of information from which we can perceive an individual's personality. Two: accuracy in personality perception can cross two distinct personality domains, which have been largely taxonomized and researched separately. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
