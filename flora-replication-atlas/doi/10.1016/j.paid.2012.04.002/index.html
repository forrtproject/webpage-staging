Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Preregistered replication of Stoeber (2012): Dyadic perfectionism in romantic relationships, Williams & Gill (2020). Outcome recorded: mixed. The findings were mixed: We were able to reproduce some of the relationships reported by Stoeber, some failed to reproduce, and some new relationships were found. Outcome read from the abstract.
