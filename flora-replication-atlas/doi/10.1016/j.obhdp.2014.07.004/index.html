Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Does Disgust Increase Unethical Behavior? A Replication of Winterich, Mittal, and Morales (2014), Kugler et al. (2020). Social Psychological and Personality Science. Outcome recorded: failed. We observe no effect of disgust on unethical behavior in any of the studies, supporting the contention that disgust has no effect on ethical decision making. Outcome read from the abstract.
