Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 2 replications of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

  • Revisiting the role of intent across moral domains: Replication and extension of Young and Saxe (2011), unknown authors (2021). OSF. Outcome recorded: mixed. We found strong empirical support for the pattern that (1) innocent intentions induce more lenient wrongness judgements for accidentals, but weak empirical support for the pattern that (2) bad intentions induce harsher wrongness judgements for failed attempts, to a significantly larger extent for harm violations compared to purity violations.
