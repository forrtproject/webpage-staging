Replications

  • Registered Replication Report, Alogna et al. (2014). Perspectives on Psychological Science. Outcome recorded: successful. These findings reveal a robust verbal overshadowing effect that is strongly influenced by the relative timing of the tasks. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
