Daniela Weismeier-Sammer

Journal of Family Business Strategy, 2011. DOI 10.1016/j.jfbs.2011.07.003

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Entrepreneurial behavior of family firms in the Indian community: adoption of a technology platform as a moderator, Chatterjee et al. (2021). Journal of Enterprising Communities: People and Places in the Global Economy. Outcome recorded: successful. Willingness to change, generational involvement, perceived technological opportunities and corporate entrepreneurship for the Indian family business community, along with strategic planning, as a moderating factor, formed the earlier model.
