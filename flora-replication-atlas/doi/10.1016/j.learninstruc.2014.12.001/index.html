The effect of speaking a minority language at home on foreign language learning

Jessica Tsimprea Maluch, Sebastian Kempert, Marko Neumann, Petra Stanat

Learning and Instruction, 2015. DOI 10.1016/j.learninstruc.2014.12.001

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • Maluch LearnInst 2015 5Awm - Ramljak/Fernández-Castilla & Santos - Secondary Data Replication, Loomas et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. In terms of statistical significance, we consider that our replication study succesfully replicates the original finding of Maluch et al. (2015). We have replicated the finding of Maluch et al. (2015) using different measures of English ability: first a compositescore, and then three different dimensions: writing, reading and listening skills. The results were very similar across dependente variables, which could be an indicator of the robustness of the results. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
