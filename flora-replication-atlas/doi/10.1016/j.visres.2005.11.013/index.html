Detection of colour changes in a moving object

Kairi Kreegipuu, Carolina Murd, Jüri Allik

Vision Research, 2006. DOI 10.1016/j.visres.2005.11.013

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Visual evoked potentials to change in coloration of a moving bar, Murd et al. (2014). Frontiers in Human Neuroscience. Outcome recorded: successful. Psychophysical results replicate the findings from the previous study showing decreased RTs to coloration changes with increase of velocity of the color changing stimulus.
