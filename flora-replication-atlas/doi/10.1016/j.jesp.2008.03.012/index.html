Judging near and distant virtue and vice

Tal Eyal, Nira Liberman, Yaacov Trope

Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 2008. DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2008.03.012

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2014.

Replications

  • Replication of Experiments Evaluating Impact of Psychological Distance on Moral Judgment, Žeželj & Jokić (2014). Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. As for the central research hypothesis, we ended up with conflicting findings: the original study found the expected impact of temporal distance to the wrongness assessment; the direct replication with the American sample yielded the opposite pattern of results, whereas direct replication with the Serbian sample yielded no significant effect.
