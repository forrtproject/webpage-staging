Journal of School Psychology, 2016. DOI 10.1016/j.jsp.2016.07.001

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Diagnostic accuracy of a universal screening multiple gating procedure: A replication study, Kilgus et al. (2018). School Psychology Quarterly. Outcome recorded: successful. Findings yielded support for the SAEBRS, with sensitivity = .93 (95% confidence interval [.89-.97]), specificity = .91 (.89-.93), and correct classification = .92. Outcome read from the abstract.
