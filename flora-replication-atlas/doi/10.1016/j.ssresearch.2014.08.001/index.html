Phillip Connor, Matthias Koenig

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • The Muslim Employment Gap, Human Capital, and Ethno-Religious Penalties: Evidence from Switzerland, Lindemann & Stolz (2018). Social Inclusion. Outcome recorded: mixed. Our key result is that Muslim affiliation does not mediate, but actually moderates, the effect of human capital on unemployment. We find a powerful interaction in that Muslims both with a very low and a very high level of education are disproportionally often unemployed. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
