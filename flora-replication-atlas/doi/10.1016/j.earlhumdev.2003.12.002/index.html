Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2021.

Replications

  • Digit ratio (2D:4D) and amniotic testosterone and estradiol: an attempted replication of Lutchmaya et al. (2004), Richards et al. (2021). Journal of Developmental Origins of Health and Disease. Outcome recorded: failed. We therefore present the findings of another study of amniotic T and E that did not find evidence for these effects at 4½-year follow-up. Outcome read from the abstract.
