Listening to action-related sentences modulates the activity of the motor system: A combined TMS and behavioral study

G. Buccino, L. Riggio, G. Melli, F. Binkofski, V. Gallese, G. Rizzolatti

Cognitive Brain Research, 2005. DOI 10.1016/j.cogbrainres.2005.02.020

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2015.

Replications

  • Does listening to action-related sentences modulate the activity of the motor system? Replication of a combined TMS and behavioral study, Gianelli & Dalla Volta (2015). Frontiers in Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. The TMS experiment showed a significant modulation of hand MEPs, but in the sense of a motor facilitation when processing hand-related verbs. On the contrary, the behavioral experiment did not show significant results. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
