Urs Fischbacher, Simon Gächter, Ernst Fehr

Economics Letters, 2001. DOI 10.1016/s0165-1765(01)00394-9

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2009.

Replications

  • Measuring conditional cooperation: a replication study in Russia, Herrmann & Thöni (2009). Experimental Economics. Outcome recorded: mixed. The share of conditional cooperators in our Russian subject pools is comparable to the one found by Fischbacher et al. in a Swiss subject pool. However, the distribution of the other types differs from the one found in Switzerland. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
