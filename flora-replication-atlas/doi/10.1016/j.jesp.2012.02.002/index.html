Andreas Kappes, Henrik Singmann, Gabriele Oettingen

Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 2012. DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2012.02.002

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Kappes JournExpSocPsych 2012 9J1 - Milyavskaya - New Data Replication, Milyavskaya et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. The moderation analysis and simple slopes revealed that when expectations were low, there was no significant difference between the mental contrasting and the irrelevant control conditions, t(306)=-.96, p=.339. Therefore, the claim was not successfully replicated. Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
