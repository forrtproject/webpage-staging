"Results from the SEM model that was used in the original study to test the study hypotheses (Model A: mediation without direct paths from leader operant behaviors to OCB) indicated that trust in the leader mediated the relationship between operant leader behaviors and employee OCB. There was a significant indirect effect of contingent reward behavior on OCB via trust: b = 0.306, SE = 0.068, z = 4.496, p = .000007, 95% CIs [0.172, 0.439] (β = 0.60).
