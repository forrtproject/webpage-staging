Neuroscience Letters, 2011. DOI 10.1016/j.neulet.2011.02.024

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2020.

Replications

  • FOXP2 expression and gray matter density in the male brains of patients with schizophrenia, Sanjuán et al. (2020). Brain Imaging and Behavior. Outcome recorded: successful. We found differences in FOXP2 expression and brain morphometry depending on the rs2396753, relating low FOXP2 mRNA levels with reduction of gray matter density. Outcome read from the abstract.
