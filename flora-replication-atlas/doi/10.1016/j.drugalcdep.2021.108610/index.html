Sex differences in circulating inflammatory mediators as a function of substance use disorder

April C. May, Kaiping Burrows, Leandra K. Figueroa-Hall, Namik Kirlic, Evan J. White, Ryan Smith, Hamed Ekhtiari, Martin P. Paulus, Jonathan Savitz, Jennifer L. Stewart

Drug and Alcohol Dependence, 2021. DOI 10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2021.108610

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • C-reactive protein concentrations diverge as a function of substance use disorder: A pre-registered replication in a clinical sample, Stewart et al. (2024). Drug and Alcohol Dependence. Outcome recorded: mixed. In contrast to the replicated CRP findings in females, regression models for IL-8 and IL-10 did not replicate, although post-hoc analyses did show that SUD+ females again exhibited higher IL-8 concentrations than SUD− females when covariates were not included. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
