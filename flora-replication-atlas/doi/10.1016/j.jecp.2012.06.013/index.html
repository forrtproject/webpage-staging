Gregory J. Poarch, Janet G. van Hell

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • Multilingual Language Control and Executive Function: A Replication Study, Poarch (2018). Frontiers in Communication. Outcome recorded: mixed. While the Simon task yielded no differences between groups, the Flanker task differed significantly across groups with multilinguals showing enhanced conflict resolution over L2 learners.
