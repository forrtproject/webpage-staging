Simultaneous ingroup and outgroup favoritism in implicit social cognition

Jordan R. Axt, Tal Moran, Yoav Bar-Anan

Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 2018. DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2018.08.007

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 1 reproduction of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful, 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Axt JournExpSocPsych 2018 zK2 - Colvin Smith - New Data Replication, Colvin et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "An independent samples t-test revealed that there is a significant difference in implicit ingroup preference between the good-focal MC-IAT D score (M = -0.25, SD = 0.47) and bad-focal MC-IATs D score (M = 0.01, SD = 0.56), t(370) = 4.97, p = .0000010255, d = 0.51. Thus, the replication of the central claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
