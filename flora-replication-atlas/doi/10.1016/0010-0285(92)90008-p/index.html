Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • Acquiring the cardinal knowledge of number words: A conceptual replication, Rousselle & Vossius (2021). Journal of Numerical Cognition. Outcome recorded: failed. Results showed that acquiring the cardinal meanings of number words spread out over a protracted period, far more extended than assumed by Wynn. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
