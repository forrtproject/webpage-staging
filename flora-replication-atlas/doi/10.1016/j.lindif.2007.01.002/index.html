Replications

  • Gender-specific effects of artificially induced gender beliefs in mental rotation, Heil et al. (2012). Learning and Individual Differences. Outcome recorded: successful. These findings suggest that gender beliefs affect men and women differently, and they also suggest that a gender belief account cannot (fully) explain gender differences in mental rotation performance.
