Replications

  • Temporal stability of the error-related negativity (ERN) and post-error positivity (Pe): The role of number of trials, Larson et al. (2010). Psychophysiology. Outcome recorded: successful. In a replication of previous results, intraclass and zero-order correlations revealed moderate to good temporal stability for participants' (N=20) grand mean ERN and Pe component amplitudes.
