Analytic thinking reduces belief in conspiracy theories

Viren Swami, Martin Voracek, Stefan Stieger, Ulrich S. Tran, Adrian Furnham

Cognition, 2014. DOI 10.1016/j.cognition.2014.08.006

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • No evidence that priming analytic thinking reduces belief in conspiracy theories: A Registered Report of high-powered direct replications of Study 2 and Study 4 from, Većkalov et al. (2024). Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. Outcome recorded: failed. We found no evidence that priming analytic thinking through the scrambled sentence task (N = 302), nor the difficult-to-read fonts (N = 488) elicited more analytic thinking, nor reduced belief in conspiracy theories. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
