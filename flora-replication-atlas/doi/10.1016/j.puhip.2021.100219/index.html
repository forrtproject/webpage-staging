Public Health in Practice, 2021. DOI 10.1016/j.puhip.2021.100219

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2022.

Replications

  • Self-perceived loneliness and depression during the Covid-19 pandemic: a two-wave replication study, Carollo et al. (2022). UCL Open Environment. Outcome recorded: mixed. Statistical analysis of depressive symptoms by week of lockdown resulted in a U-shaped pattern between weeks 3 and 7 of Wave 1 of the UK national lockdown. Outcome read from the abstract. View paper
