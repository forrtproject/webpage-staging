The vision heuristic: Judging music ensembles by sight alone

Chia-Jung Tsay

Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 2014. DOI 10.1016/j.obhdp.2013.10.003

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2019.

Replications

  • Tsay OrgBehavior 2014 exd7 - Wilbiks - New Data Replication, Wilbiks et al. (2019). OSF. Outcome recorded: failed. "The claim selected for the original study yielded a positive Cohen's d in the focal test, corresponding to participants identifying the winning group from each competition at a rate significantly greater than chance. The replication yielded a positive Cohen's d in the focal test, corresponding to an effect in the same pattern compared to the original study. These results mean that the claim with regard to the focal hypothesis (H*) did not replicate." Outcome read from OSF report. View paper
