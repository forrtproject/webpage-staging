Antipsychotic use and risk of breast cancer in women with schizophrenia: a nationwide nested case-control study in Finland

Heidi Taipale, Marco Solmi, Markku Lähteenvuo, Antti Tanskanen, Christoph U Correll, Jari Tiihonen

The Lancet Psychiatry, 2021. DOI 10.1016/s2215-0366(21)00241-8

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2024.

Replications

  • Antipsychotic Use and Risk of Breast Cancer in Women With Severe Mental Illness: Replication of a Nationwide Nested Case–Control Database Study, Solmi et al. (2024). Schizophrenia Bulletin. Outcome recorded: successful. These findings are consistent with those of a similar nested case–control study previously conducted in Finland, in which the exposure to prolactin-increasing antipsychotics for ≥5 years was associated with a 56% overall increased odds of breast cancer, as well as increased odds for both ductal (aOR = 1.42) and lobular adenocar-cinoma (aOR = 2.36), in women with schizophrenia.
