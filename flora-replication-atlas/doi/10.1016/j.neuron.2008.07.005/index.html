Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • Feasibility of unconscious instrumental conditioning: A registered replication, Skora et al. (2023). Cortex. Outcome recorded: mixed. We found that apparent unconscious learning took place when replicating the original methods directly and according to the tests of awareness used. However, we could not establish that the full sample was unaware in a separate awareness check.
