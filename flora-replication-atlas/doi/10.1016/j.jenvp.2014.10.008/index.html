How far is your money from your mouth? The effects of intrinsic relative to extrinsic values on willingness to pay and protect the environment

Lisbeth Ku, Charles Zaroff

Journal of Environmental Psychology, 2014. DOI 10.1016/j.jenvp.2014.10.008

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication and 2 reproductions of this study. Recorded outcomes: 2 failed, 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2025.

Replications

  • SCORE new data replication of Ku & Zaroff (2014), Tyner et al. (2025). OSF. Outcome recorded: successful. "The mean WTP in the intrinsic [high_ie] group (0.1887432, n = 235) was statistically significantly higher than the mean WTP in the extrinsic [low_ie] group (-0.1627251, n = 219): t = 3.763, df = 446.26, p = 0.0001903. Thus, this replication of the claim was successful according to the SCORE criteria. The Cohen's D effect size is 0.3511505." Outcome read from OSF report.

Reproductions

  • Reproduction (with author data): Ku & Zaroff (2014, Journal of Environmental Psychology), Parsons et al. (2022). OSF. Outcome recorded: computational issues, not checked. Computational: Reproduction result for single-trace: t = -5.12 (vs original t=4.87, wrong sign); for m7k461: standardized path = .89 (vs original .54). Both claims reported "did not reproduce" but real comparable numbers were obtained in each case. Outcome read from Computational: Reproduction outcome statements + TABLE 0/TABLE 1. View paper
  • Reproduction (with author data): Ku & Zaroff (2014, Journal of Environmental Psychology), Sonmez et al. (2020). OSF. Outcome recorded: computational issues, not checked. Computational: "The reproduction attempt was successful in general, with minor deviations from the reported values... The difference between the groups was significant in the reported direction... All of the above-mentioned deviations were due to the original authors' mistake in recoding the reverse item" Outcome read from Computational: Reproduction result. View paper
