Has this study been replicated?

Replications

  • Double misinformation and eyewitness performance: An experimental replication, Smołka & Blank (2024). Applied Cognitive Psychology. Outcome recorded: mixed. This supports (at least partly, and in an item analysis) the main finding presented by Blank et al. (2022) that MA negatively correlates with misin- formation effect change, that is, at high levels of MA, double misinformation results in a reduction of the misinformation effect (compared to single misinformation), but at low levels of MA in an increase.
