Hormonal contraceptive congruency: Implications for relationship jealousy

Kelly D. Cobey, S. Craig Roberts, Abraham P. Buunk

Personality and Individual Differences, 2013. DOI 10.1016/j.paid.2013.04.031

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 failed. The earliest on record is from 2018.

Replications

  • A high-powered replication study finds no effect of starting or stopping hormonal contraceptive use on relationship quality, Jern et al. (2018). Evolution and Human Behavior. Outcome recorded: failed. Despite excellent statistical power (≥98.7%), and close procedural replications of two out of three previous studies (Cobey et al., 2013; Roberts, Little, et al., 2014), we did not find support for the HC congruency hypothesis.
