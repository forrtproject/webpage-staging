Christoph Zangger, Janine Widmer

Early Childhood Research Quarterly, 2020. DOI 10.1016/j.ecresq.2019.10.004

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2023.

Replications

  • Eleven years of student replication projects provide evidence on the correlates of replicability in psychology, Boyce et al. (2023). Royal Society Open Science. Outcome recorded: successful. My key test was the odds ratio associated with the amount that subsidies change the choice towards a child care type, the first row in model 1, in the confirmatory analysis section above. In the authors original model, they found an odds ratio of 1.025, meaning that a unit increase in subsidies for the corresponding childcare alternative is associated with a 2.5% increase in the odds of preferring that mode of childcare. My experimental data produced a fairly similar result… Outcome read from summary of report. View paper
