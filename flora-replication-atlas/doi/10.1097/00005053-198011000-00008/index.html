Replications

  • Verbal Impairment after Closed Head Injury Report of a Replication Study, SARNO (1984). The Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease. Outcome recorded: successful. This study confirms the observation that all CHI postcoma patients suffer important, disabling verbal changes, however mild or apparent, which persist up to 1 year post-trauma.
