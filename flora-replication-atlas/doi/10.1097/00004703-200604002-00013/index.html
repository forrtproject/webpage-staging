Replications

  • Sustainability of Early Intensive Behavioral Intervention for Children With Autism Spectrum Disorder in a Community Setting, Waters et al. (2018). Behavior Modification. Outcome recorded: successful. Linear mixed models indicated that EIBI participants improved significantly more than SAU participants on standardized tests of IQ, nonverbal IQ, adaptive behavior, and academic achievement, administered by independent evaluators. [...] the study provides evidence for the sustainability of effective EIBI in community settings for children with ASD who start intervention at varying ages throughout early childhood.
