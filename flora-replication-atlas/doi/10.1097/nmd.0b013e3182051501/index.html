Is Torture Reliably Assessed and a Valid Indicator of Poor Mental Health?

Michael Hollifield, Teddy D. Warner, Joseph Westermeyer

Journal of Nervous & Mental Disease, 2011. DOI 10.1097/nmd.0b013e3182051501

Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 successful. The earliest on record is from 2013.

Replications

  • The Mental Health Effects of Torture Trauma and Its Severity: A Replication and Extension, Kira et al. (2013). Psychology. Outcome recorded: successful. Partial correlation and hierarchical regression results found that torture trauma and torture severity were not significant predictors of PTSD, other cumulative life traumas that happened before and after torture were the highly significant predictors. These results replicated previous findings of Kira et al., 2006; and Hollifield et al., 2011. Outcome read from the discussion. View paper
