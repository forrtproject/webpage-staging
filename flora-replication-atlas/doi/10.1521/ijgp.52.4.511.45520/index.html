Has this study been replicated?

The atlas records 1 replication of this study. Recorded outcomes: 1 mixed. The earliest on record is from 2010.

Replications

  • Interpersonal predictors of outcome following short‐term group therapy for complicated grief: a replication, Joyce et al. (2010). Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy. Outcome recorded: mixed. Only the patient's attachment insecurity emerged as a strong predictor of psychotherapy outcome, providing a replication of the key finding from the previous study. Outcome read from the abstract.
